# Kemmerer Unit 1 Natrium 발전소 개요 (Kemmerer Unit 1 Natrium Plant Overview)

> [!abstract] 요약
> Kemmerer Unit 1은 TerraPower가 공급하는 `840 MWt` pool-type sodium-cooled fast reactor%%t:SFR%%인 Natrium 원자로이며, 신청자는 `10 CFR Part 50`에 따른 construction permit%%t:CP%%을 신청한다. molten salt 에너지 저장 계통은 원자로 출력을 일정하게 유지하면서 전력망 공급을 최대 `500 MWe`(net)까지 바꿀 수 있게 한다.

## 원문 사실
- "US SFR Owner, LLC (USO), a subsidiary of TerraPower, LLC, is requesting approval for a construction permit of an 840 megawatt thermal (MWt) Natrium Reactor Plant, Kemmerer Power Station Unit 1 (Kemmerer Unit 1), to be located in Lincoln County, Wyoming approximately 3 miles (4.8 km) southwest of Kemmerer, Wyoming." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1 p.19]
- "Kemmerer Unit 1 is being licensed under Title 10 of the Code of Federal Regulations (10 CFR) Part 50, “Domestic Licensing of Production and Utilization Facilities.”" [PSAR_ch01 §1.1 p.19]
- "This preliminary safety analysis report (PSAR) includes the information required by 10 CFR 50.34(a) to be provided as part of an application for a construction permit and demonstrates that the plant can be constructed and operated at the proposed location without undue risk to the health and safety of the public in accordance with 10 CFR 50.35(a)." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1 p.19]
- "Kemmerer Unit 1 is a Natrium reactor supplied by TerraPower." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.1 p.19]
- "Kemmerer Unit 1 will be constructed and operated as part of participation in the U.S. Department of Energy's (DOE) Advanced Reactor Demonstration Program (ARDP) to demonstrate the Natrium technology and will provide electricity generation capacity in the PacifiCorp service area." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.2 p.19]
- "The nature and inventory of contained radioactive materials is provided in Section 3.2." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.2 p.19]
- "The Natrium reactor plant is an 840 MWt pool-type sodium fast reactor (SFR) that contains a compact and simple safety envelope and a molten salt energy storage system which enables the plant to vary its supply of energy to the grid, up to 500 megawatts electric (MWe) net, while maintaining constant reactor power." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.3 p.19]
- "The reactor operates near atmospheric pressure, circulating sodium through its core with pumps." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.3 p.19]
- "Heat is transferred from the primary loop within the Primary Heat Transport System (PHT) to an intermediate sodium piping loop within the Intermediate Heat Transport System (IHT) via intermediate heat exchangers located in the reactor pool." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.3 p.19]
- "The IHT transports reactor heat from the intermediate heat exchangers to the Nuclear Island (NI) Salt System (NSS) via sodium-salt heat exchangers, which transport heat to the Energy Island (EI) hot salt storage tank for thermal energy storage." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.3 p.19]
- "The heat stored in the hot salt tank is used to generate steam for use in commercially available steam turbine generators." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.3 p.19]
- "The design includes reliable inherent and passive safety features including near-atmospheric operating pressures, always-on passive air cooling (Figure 1.1-3), and inherent reactivity feedback, which are summarized in Figure 1.1-4 and described in further detail in Section 1.1.4." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.3 p.20]
- "Kemmerer Unit 1 comprises an Energy Island and a Nuclear Island as described in Section 1.1.3." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4 p.20]
- "Further information on safety-related (SR) and non-safety-related with special treatment (NSRST) systems is provided in Chapter 7." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4 p.20]
- "The Solid Radwaste Processing System and Liquid Radwaste Processing System descriptions are found in Chapter 9." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4 p.20]
- "The reactor is a metal fueled, pool-type, SFR." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.1 p.20]
- "Safety functions are made integral to the reactor vessel, and support equipment is moved to separate structures, resulting in a simplified reactor building." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.1 p.20]
- "The reactor uses sodium-bonded metallic fuel with high chromium martensitic steel (HT9) cladding." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.1 p.20]
- "The intermediate sodium loop transfers heat to the NSS via Sodium-Salt Heat Exchangers (SHXs) for normal steady state operations, and to the atmosphere via the Intermediate Air Cooling System (IAC) sodium-air heat exchanger during startup and shutdown." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.1 p.20]

## 해설
`US SFR Owner, LLC`는 `TerraPower, LLC`의 자회사로, Wyoming주 Lincoln County의 Kemmerer 남서쪽에 들어설 Kemmerer Power Station Unit 1의 CP%%t:CP%% 승인을 신청한다. 발전소는 `10 CFR Part 50`에 따라 인허가되며, 이 `PSAR`은 `10 CFR 50.34(a)`가 요구하는 정보를 담고 `10 CFR 50.35(a)`에 따라 공중의 건강과 안전에 과도한 위험 없이 제안 부지에서 건설·운전될 수 있음을 보인다. Kemmerer Unit 1은 TerraPower가 공급하는 Natrium 원자로이며, `U.S. Department of Energy`의 Advanced Reactor Demonstration Program에 참여해 Natrium 기술을 실증하고 PacifiCorp 공급 지역에 발전 용량을 제공한다. 내장 방사성 물질의 성질과 재고량은 `Section 3.2`에 있다.

Natrium 원자로 발전소는 `840 MWt` pool-type SFR%%t:SFR%%로, 간결하고 단순한 safety envelope과 molten salt 에너지 저장 계통을 갖추어 원자로 출력을 일정하게 유지하면서 전력망 공급을 최대 `500 MWe`(net)까지 바꿀 수 있다. 원자로는 대기압 근처에서 운전하며 펌프로 노심에 소듐을 순환시킨다. 원자로 pool 안의 intermediate heat exchanger가 Primary Heat Transport System의 primary 루프에서 Intermediate Heat Transport System의 중간 소듐 배관 루프로 열을 옮기고, Intermediate Heat Transport System은 sodium-salt heat exchanger를 거쳐 Nuclear Island Salt System으로 열을 보내며, 열은 Energy Island의 고온 염 저장 탱크로 옮겨져 열에너지로 저장된다. 고온 염 탱크의 열은 상용 증기 터빈 발전기용 증기를 만드는 데 쓰인다. 중간 소듐 루프는 정상 운전의 정상상태에서는 sodium-salt heat exchanger로 Nuclear Island Salt System에, 기동과 정지 중에는 Intermediate Air Cooling System의 sodium-air heat exchanger로 대기에 열을 전달한다.

설계는 대기압에 가까운 운전 압력, 항상 작동하는 피동 공기 냉각, 고유 반응도 되먹임 같은 신뢰성 있는 고유·피동 안전 특성을 포함한다. 원자로는 금속 핵연료를 쓰는 pool-type SFR%%t:SFR%%이고, 핵연료는 high chromium martensitic steel(`HT9`) 피복의 sodium-bonded 금속 핵연료이다. 안전 기능을 원자로 용기에 통합하고 지원 설비를 별도 구조물로 옮겨 원자로 건물을 단순화했다. Kemmerer Unit 1은 Energy Island와 Nuclear Island로 이루어지며, Safety-Related%%t:SR%%과 Non-Safety-Related with Special Treatment%%t:NSRST%% 계통의 상세는 `Chapter 7`에, 고체·액체 방사성폐기물 처리 계통은 `Chapter 9`에 있다.
