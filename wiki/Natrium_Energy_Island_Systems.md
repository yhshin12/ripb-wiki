# Natrium Energy Island 계통 (Natrium Energy Island Systems)

> [!abstract] 요약
> `PSAR` §1.1.4.2는 2차 계통이 원자로에서 낸 열을 전력으로 바꾸는 경로를 `NSS`–`ESS`–`TSS`–`SGS` 염 순환과 동력 사이클로 서술하고, 뒤따르는 절들이 서술하는 Energy Island 계통과 기능을 모두 Non-Safety-Related with No Special Treatment%%t:NST%%로 분류한다.
> Main Power는 230 kilovolt 계통과 정상·대체 주전원 공급 계통의 세 하위 계통으로 이루어지고 소내 보조부하는 6.9 kV 배전계통으로 공급된다.
> Energy Island 보조 전력계통에는 `EMV`, `ELV`, `EDC`, `EUP`, `EHT`가 있으며, Standby Diesel Generator는 `ESP`에 속하고 loss of offsite power 때 Nuclear Island와 Energy Island의 특정 부하에 예비전원을 준다.

## 원문 사실
- "The secondary systems and components transform the heat generated within the reactor into electrical power." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2 p.28]
- "The reactor operates at near atmospheric pressure, circulating sodium coolant through its core, with heat transferred from the primary sodium coolant (via PHT in-vessel pumps and in-vessel heat exchangers) to an intermediate sodium loop (the IHT)." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2 p.28]
- "The intermediate sodium loop transfers heat to the NSS via sodium to salt heat exchangers." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2 p.28]
- "The NSS transfers heat to the ESS and to the Thermal Salt Storage System (TSS) hot tank." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2 p.28]
- "The salt stored in the TSS hot tank is pumped through the steam generators and sent to the TSS cold tank." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2 p.28]
- "The steam generator equipment converts water into steam by passing the hot salt through an economizer (water preheater), evaporator, superheater, and reheater." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2 p.28]
- "The steam generator provides steam to the turbine to produce power." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2 p.28]
- "As described in Reference 1.1-1, the NI boundary conditions have been intentionally designed so the interface with the EI does not impact the Kemmerer Unit 1 safety analysis." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2 p.28]
- "Similarly, steam generation and thermal energy storage operations (e.g., ramp rates) are independent from reactor power operations due to the presence of the molten salt energy storage tanks." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2 p.28]
- "All EI systems and functions described in the following sections are classified as NST." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2 p.28]
- "Heat is removed from the IHT SHXs in the RAB by the NSS to ESS to TSS molten salt loop during reactor operation." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.1 p.28]
- "This salt is then stored in the TSS molten salt storage tanks to reserve thermal energy to accommodate fluctuations in steam turbine generator output." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.1 p.28]
- "Cold salt from the TSS cold salt storage flows through the ESS to NSS cold salt piping via the ESS cold salt transfer pumps, across the EI and NI boundary, to the IHT SHXs." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.1 p.28]
- "Hot salt from the SHXs flows through the NSS to ESS hot salt piping back across the NI and EI boundary to the TSS hot salt storage." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.1 p.28]
- "The hot salt is pumped from the TSS hot salt storage through the Steam Generation System (SGS) to transfer heat from the salt to water to create steam." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.1 p.28]
- "Cold salt is returned from the SGS to the TSS cold salt storage." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.1 p.28]
- "The NSS isolation valves outside of the RAB form the boundary between the NSS and ESS systems." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.1 p.29]
- "The NSS monitors molten salt flow rate and the hot and cold salt temperatures and pressures." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.1 p.29]
- "This provides feedback to the MCR that the NSS to ESS to TSS to SGS loop is transferring heat to the EI from the NI during normal operations." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.1 p.29]
- "The NSS to ESS to TSS to SGS systems form the salt loop that transfers heat from the NI to the EI." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.1 p.29]
- "Heat is removed from the TSS salt loop via the SGS." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.2 p.29]
- "Salt flows through the SGS heat exchangers to produce superheated and reheated steam." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.2 p.29]
- "The SGS receives high pressure feedwater from the Condensate and Feedwater System (CFW) for steam generation." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.2 p.29]
- "The Steam Turbine System (STS) converts steam into rotational energy." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.2 p.29]
- "The Generator System (GEN) converts the rotational energy into electricity." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.2 p.29]
- "The CFW condenses waste steam from the STS in the condenser." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.2 p.29]
- "Condensate is pumped through several low pressure feedwater heaters to the deaerator." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.2 p.29]
- "The feedwater pumps take suction from the deaerator and supply high pressure feedwater to the SGS." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.2 p.29]
- "The Heat Rejection System (HRS) removes heat from the condenser through the cooling tower." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.2 p.29]
- "The Main Power is the main power system for Kemmerer Unit 1 and consists of three subsystems:" [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.3 p.29]
- "The 230 kilovolt (kV) power subsystem that transfers power between Kemmerer Unit 1 and the 230 kV transmission lines" [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.3 p.29]
- "A normal main power supply subsystem that transforms and transfers the power generated by the steam-turbine generator unit to the 230 kV switchyard, and supplies plant auxiliary loads through the unit auxiliary transformer during plant normal operation" [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.3 p.29]
- "An alternate main power supply subsystem that transforms and transfers power from the 230 kV switchyard to the plant auxiliary loads through the reserve auxiliary transformer when the normal main power supply subsystem is out of service" [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.3 p.29]
- "During normal plant operation, the normal main power supply subsystem transmits generated power from the main generator to the 230 kV switchyard through the generator circuit breaker and the main step-up transformer." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.3 p.29]
- "Plant auxiliary power is provided from the isolated phase bus tapped connection through the unit auxiliary transformers, which, in turn, supplies the medium voltage 6.9 kV distribution system." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.3 p.29]
- "During plant startup and shutdown, with the generator circuit breaker open, plant auxiliary loads are back fed power via the main step-up transformer from the 230 kV switchyard to the unit auxiliary transformers." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.3 p.29]
- "When the normal main power supply system is not available, plant auxiliary loads are supplied from the reserve auxiliary transformer, an alternate main power supply system, fed from the 230 kV switchyard." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.2.3 p.29]
- "The EI auxiliary electrical system includes the EI AC Electrical Power Medium Voltage System (EMV), EI AC Electrical Power Low Voltage System (ELV), EI DC power supply system (EDC), EI Uninterruptible AC Power Supply System (EUP), and EI Heat Tracing System (EHT)." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.3.14 p.34]
- "Under normal operating conditions, the EI auxiliary electrical system supplies power to EI equipment required for plant startup, normal operation, and shutdown." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.3.14 p.34]
- "This power is distributed through EMV, ELV, EDC, EUP, and EHT." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.3.14 p.34]
- "Power for plant auxiliary loads in the EI is provided normally through the unit auxiliary transformer at the 6.9 kV level." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.3.14 p.34]
- "A reserve auxiliary transformer is provided as an alternate source of power in case of failure of unit auxiliary transformer." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.3.14 p.34]
- "During a loss of offsite power event, the SDGs are automatically started and provide backup power to NMV for plant-specific loads and the EMV for plant-specific loads for investment protection." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.3.14 p.34]
- "SDGs also supply power to NI and EI battery chargers, and heat tracing to avoid freezing." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.3.14 p.34]
- "The SDGs are part of the ESP." [PSAR_ch01 §1.1.4.3.14 p.34]

## 해설
열은 원자로에서 1차 나트륨과 중간 나트륨 루프를 거쳐 `NSS`로, 다시 `ESS`와 `TSS` hot tank로 간다. `TSS` hot tank의 염은 증기발생기를 지나 cold tank로 돌아오고, 증기발생기 설비는 뜨거운 염을 economizer(급수 예열기), evaporator, superheater, reheater로 통과시켜 물을 증기로 바꾼다. `NSS`의 격리밸브가 Reactor Auxiliary Building 밖에서 `NSS`와 `ESS`의 경계를 이룬다. 원문은 뒤따르는 절들에서 서술하는 Energy Island 계통과 기능이 모두 NST%%t:NST%%라고 적고, Nuclear Island 경계조건이 Energy Island와의 접속이 Kemmerer Unit 1 안전해석에 영향을 주지 않도록 의도적으로 설계되었다고 적는다. 용융염 저장탱크 덕분에 증기 생산과 열에너지 저장의 운전은 원자로 출력 운전과 독립이다.

동력 사이클은 `SGS`가 염에서 열을 받아 과열·재열 증기를 만들고, `CFW`가 고압 급수를 공급하며, `STS`가 증기를 회전에너지로, `GEN`이 회전에너지를 전기로 바꾸는 구성이다. `CFW`는 복수기에서 폐증기를 응축하고, 응축수는 저압 급수가열기를 거쳐 deaerator로 간다. `HRS`는 냉각탑을 통해 복수기의 열을 버린다.

Main Power는 세 하위 계통이다. 230 kilovolt 계통은 Kemmerer Unit 1과 송전선 사이에서 전력을 주고받고, 정상 주전원 공급 계통은 발전기 전력을 230 kV 스위치야드로 보내면서 unit auxiliary transformer로 소내 보조부하를 공급하며, 대체 주전원 공급 계통은 정상 계통이 정지했을 때 reserve auxiliary transformer로 스위치야드에서 보조부하에 공급한다. 소내 보조 전력은 isolated phase bus의 분기에서 unit auxiliary transformer를 거쳐 6.9 kV 배전계통으로 간다. 기동·정지 때는 발전기 차단기를 연 채 main step-up transformer를 통해 역송전으로 보조부하에 공급한다.

Energy Island 보조 전력계통은 정상 운전 조건에서 기동·운전·정지에 필요한 Energy Island 기기에 전력을 준다. loss of offsite power 때에는 Standby Diesel Generator가 자동 기동해 투자 보호 목적으로 `NMV`와 `EMV`의 특정 부하에, 그리고 Nuclear Island와 Energy Island의 축전지 충전기와 동결 방지 heat tracing에 전력을 준다. 이 발전기는 `ESP`에 속한다.
