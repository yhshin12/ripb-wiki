# SFR-DC 반응도 제어 (SFR-DC Reactivity Control, Criteria 26–29)

> [!abstract] 요약
> 기준 26–29는 반응도 제어 계통, 결합 능력, 반응도 한계, Anticipated Operational Occurrence%%t:AOO%% 방호를 다룬다. 기준 26은 general design criteria%%t:GDC%%의 두 기준을 합친 advanced reactor design criteria를 그대로 따르며, 제어봉 대신 means라는 말로 최소 두 반응도 제어 계통이나 수단이 네 가지 능력을 제공하게 한다. 기준 27은 삭제되어 기준 26에 통합되었고, 기준 28은 primary coolant boundary 용어로 바뀌고 가정 반응도 사고 목록이 지워졌다.

## 원문 사실
- "A minimum of two reactivity control systems or means shall provide:" [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 26 p.74]
- "(1) A means of inserting negative reactivity at a sufficient rate and amount to assure, with appropriate margin for malfunctions, that the design limits for the fission product barriers are not exceeded and safe shutdown is achieved and maintained during normal operation, including anticipated operational occurrences." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 26 p.74]
- "(2) A means which is independent and diverse from the other(s), shall be capable of controlling the rate of reactivity changes resulting from planned, normal power changes to assure that the design limits for the fission product barriers are not exceeded." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 26 p.74]
- "(3) A means of inserting negative reactivity at a sufficient rate and amount to assure, with appropriate margin for malfunctions, that the capability to cool the core is maintained and a means of shutting down the reactor and maintaining, at a minimum, a safe shutdown condition following a postulated accident." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 26 p.74]
- "(4) A means for holding the reactor shutdown under conditions which allow for interventions such as fuel loading, inspection and repair shall be provided." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 26 p.75]
- "Recent licensing activity, associated with the application of GDC 26 and GDC 27 to new reactor designs (ADAMS Accession Nos. ML16116A083 (Ref. 29) and ML16292A589) (Ref. 30), revealed that additional clarity could be provided in the area of reactivity control requirements. ARDC 26 combines the scope of GDC 26 and GDC 27. The development of ARDC 26 is informed by the proposed general design criteria of 1965 (AEC-R 2/49, November 5), 1967 (32 FR 10216) (Ref. 31), current GDC 26 and 27, the definition of safety-related SSC in 10 CFR 50.2, SECY-94-084, “Policy and Technical Issues Associated with the Regulatory Treatment of Non-Safety Systems in Passive Plant Designs” (Ref. 32), and the prior application of reactivity control requirements." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 26 p.74]
- "(1) Currently the second sentence of GDC 26 states, that one of the reactivity control systems shall use control rods and shall be capable of reliably controlling reactivity changes to ensure that, under conditions of normal operation, including AOOs, and with appropriate margin for malfunctions such as stuck rods, specified acceptable fuel design limits are not exceeded. The staff recognizes that specifying control rods may not be suitable for advanced reactors. Additionally, reliably controlling reactivity, as applied to GDC 26, has been interpreted as ensuring the control rods are capable of rapidly (i.e., within a few seconds) shutting down the reactor (ADAMS Accession No. ML16292A589) (Ref. 30)." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 26 p.74]
- "The staff changed control rods to “means” in recognition that advanced reactor designs may not rely on control rods to rapidly shut down the reactor (e.g., alternative system designs or inherent feedback mechanisms may be relied upon to perform this function). The wording of “reliably controlling reactivity” in GDC 26 has been replaced with “inserting negative reactivity at a sufficient rate and amount” to more clearly define the requirement. For a non-LWR design the rate of negative reactivity insertion may not necessitate rapid (within seconds) insertion but should occur in a time frame such that the fission product barrier design limits are not exceeded." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 26 p.75]
- "The term “specified acceptable fuel design limits” is replaced with “design limits for fission product barriers” to be consistent with the AOO acceptance criteria while also addressing liquid fueled reactors which may not have SAFDLs. ARDC 10 and ARDC 15 provide the appropriate design limits for the fuel and reactor coolant boundary, respectively." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 26 p.75]
- "The wording “safe shutdown is achieved and maintained…” has been added again to more clearly define the requirements associated with reliably controlling reactivity in GDC 26. SECY-94-084, “Policy and Technical Issues Associated with the Regulatory Treatment of Non-Safety Systems in Passive Plant Designs” (Ref. 32), describes the characteristics of a safe shutdown condition as reactor subcriticality, decay heat removal, and radioactive materials containment. ARDC 26 (1) clearly defines that reactor shutdown at any time during the transient is the performance requirement. The distinction between during and following the transient is discussed in (2) below." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 26 p.75]
- "In regards to safety class, the capability to insert negative reactivity at a rate and amount to preserve the fission product barrier(s) and to shut down the reactor during an AOO is identified as a function performed by safety-related SSCs in the 10 CFR 50.2 definition of safety-related SSCs." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 26 p.75]
- "(2) The first sentence of GDC 26, states that two independent reactivity control systems of different design principles shall be provided. The third sentence of GDC 26, states that the second reactivity control system shall be capable of reliably controlling the rate of changes resulting from planned, normal power changes (including xenon burnout) to assure specified acceptable fuel design limits are not exceeded. ARDC 26 (2) is consistent with the current requirements of the second reactivity control system specified in GDC 26. The words “including xenon burnout” have been deleted as this may not be as important for some non-LWR reactor designs. Also, “of different design principles” from the first sentence of GDC 26 has been replaced with “independent and diverse” to clarify the requirement. The reactivity means given by ARDC 26 (2) is a system important to safety but not necessarily safety-related as it does not mitigate an AOO or accident but is used to control planned, normal reactivity changes such that the design limits for the fission product barriers are preserved thereby minimizing challenges to the safety-related reactivity control means or protection system." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 26 p.76]
- "The term “independent and diverse” indicates no shared systems or components and a design which is different enough such that no common failure modes exist between the system or means in ARDC 26 (2) and safety-related systems in ARDC 26 (1) and (3)." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 26 p.76]
- "(3) Current GDC 27 states that the reactivity control systems shall be designed to have a combined capability of reliably controlling reactivity changes to assure that under postulated accident conditions and with appropriate margin for stuck rods the capability to cool the core is maintained. Reliably controlling reactivity, as applied to GDC 27 requires that the reactor achieve and maintain a safe, stable condition, including subcriticality, using only safety related equipment with margin for stuck rods (ADAMS Accession No. ML16116A083) (Ref. 29)." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 26 p.76]
- "ARDC 26 (3) is written to clarify that shut down following a postulated accident using safety-related equipment or means is required. The term “following a postulated accident” refers to the time when plant parameters are relatively stable, no additional means of mitigation are needed and margins to acceptance criteria are constant or increasing. ARDC 26 allows for a return to power during a postulated accident consistent with the current licensing basis of some existing PWRs if sufficient heat removal capability exists (e.g., PWR main steam line break accident), but ARDC 26 (1) precludes a return to power during an AOO." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 26 p.77]
- "(4) The fourth sentence of GDC 26 regarding the capability to reach cold shutdown has been generalized in ARDC 26 (4) to refer to activities which are performed at conditions below (less limiting than) those normally associated with safe shutdown. SECY-94-084 (Ref. 32) describes staff positions on obtaining a cold shutdown and explains that the requirement to bring the plant to cold shutdown is driven by the need to inspect and repair a plant following an accident. In regards to safety class, the capability to bring the plant to a cold shutdown is not covered by the definition of safety-related SSCs in 10 CFR 50.2, and most operating pressurized-water reactors have not credited safety-related SSCs to satisfy this requirement of GDC 26. Based on the information provided above, the system credited for holding the reactor subcritical under conditions necessary for activities such as refueling, inspection and repair is identified as an important to safety system." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 26 p.77]
- "DELETED—Information incorporated into SFR-DC 26" [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 27 p.78]
- "DELETED—Information incorporated into ARDC 26" [RG_1.232 ARDC 27 p.42]
- "The reactivity control systems shall be designed with appropriate limits on the potential amount and rate of reactivity increase to ensure that the effects of postulated reactivity accidents can neither (1) result in damage to the primary coolant boundary greater than limited local yielding nor (2) sufficiently disturb the core, its support structures or other reactor vessel internals to impair significantly the capability to cool the core." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 28 p.78]
- "“Reactor coolant pressure boundary” has been relabeled as “primary coolant boundary” to conform to standard terms used in the LMR industry. The use of the term “primary” indicates that the SFR-DC are applicable to the primary cooling system, not the intermediate cooling system." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 28 p.78]
- "The list of “postulated reactivity accidents” has been deleted." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 28 p.78]
- "The reactivity control systems shall be designed with appropriate limits on the potential amount and rate of reactivity increase to ensure that the effects of postulated reactivity accidents can neither (1) result in damage to the reactor coolant boundary greater than limited local yielding nor (2) sufficiently disturb the core, its support structures, or other reactor vessel internals to impair significantly the capability to cool the core." [RG_1.232 ARDC 28 p.42]
- "The protection and reactivity control systems shall be designed to assure an extremely high probability of accomplishing their safety functions in the event of anticipated operational occurrences." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 29 p.78]

## 해설
기준 26은 최소 두 반응도 제어 계통이나 수단이 네 가지를 제공하도록 요구한다. (1)은 오작동에 대한 적절한 여유를 두고, AOO%%t:AOO%%를 포함한 정상 운전에서 핵분열생성물 barrier%%t:BARRIER%%의 설계 한계를 넘지 않으면서 안전 정지를 달성·유지할 만큼 충분한 속도와 양으로 음의 반응도를 넣는 수단이다. (2)는 다른 수단과 independent and diverse하며, 계획된 정상 출력 변화에 따른 반응도 변화 속도를 제어해 설계 한계를 넘지 않게 하는 수단이다. (3)은 오작동 여유를 두고 노심 냉각 능력을 유지하며 가정 사고 뒤 적어도 안전 정지 상태를 달성·유지할 음의 반응도 삽입 수단이다. (4)는 핵연료 장전, 검사, 수리 같은 개입이 가능한 조건에서 원자로를 정지 상태로 유지하는 수단이다.

근거에 따르면 최근 인허가 경험에서 반응도 제어 요건을 더 명확히 할 필요가 드러나, advanced reactor design criteria의 이 기준이 GDC%%t:GDC%%의 26과 27의 범위를 합쳤다. 제어봉 명시는 신형로에 맞지 않을 수 있어 means로 바꿨고, reliably controlling reactivity는 요건을 명확히 하려고 inserting negative reactivity at a sufficient rate and amount로 바꿨다. 비경수형원전(non-light-water reactor)%%t:NON_LWR%% 설계의 음의 반응도 삽입은 수 초 안의 빠른 삽입이 필요하지 않을 수 있지만 핵분열생성물 barrier%%t:BARRIER%% 설계 한계를 넘지 않는 시간 안에 이루어져야 한다. specified acceptable fuel design limits%%t:SAFDL%%는 AOO%%t:AOO%% 수용 기준과 일관되게 하면서 SAFDL%%t:SAFDL%%이 없을 수 있는 액체 연료 원자로도 다루도록 design limits for fission product barriers로 바꿨다. 근거는 AOO%%t:AOO%% 중 원자로를 정지하고 핵분열생성물 barrier%%t:BARRIER%%를 보존하는 반응도 삽입 능력을 10 CFR 50.2의 safety-related Structures, Systems, and Components%%t:SSC%% 정의에 속하는 기능으로 본다. (2)의 수단은 AOO%%t:AOO%%나 사고를 완화하지 않고 계획된 정상 반응도 변화를 제어하는 데 쓰이므로 important to safety 계통이지만 반드시 safety-related는 아니며, 그 결과 safety-related 반응도 제어 수단이나 보호 계통에 대한 도전을 줄인다. independent and diverse는 공유하는 계통이나 기기가 없고 (1), (3)의 safety-related 계통과 공통 고장 모드가 없을 만큼 설계가 다르다는 뜻이다. following a postulated accident는 발전소 변수가 비교적 안정되고 추가 완화 수단이 필요 없으며 수용 기준에 대한 여유가 일정하거나 커지는 시점을 뜻한다. 기준은 충분한 열제거 능력이 있으면 가정 사고 중 출력 복귀를 허용하지만, AOO%%t:AOO%% 중 출력 복귀는 (1)이 막는다. (4)의 저온 정지 능력은 10 CFR 50.2의 safety-related 정의에 포함되지 않으므로, 재장전·검사·수리 조건에서 원자로를 미임계로 유지하는 계통은 important to safety 계통으로 본다.

기준 27은 삭제되어 기준 26에 통합되었고, 표 1의 상태 `Same as ARDC`는 advanced reactor design criteria의 같은 번호 조문도 삭제되었다는 사실과 맞는다. 기준 28은 반응도 제어 계통에 잠재적 반응도 증가량과 증가 속도의 적절한 한계를 두어, 가정 반응도 사고의 영향이 primary coolant boundary에 제한된 국부 항복을 넘는 손상을 주거나 노심·지지 구조물·원자로 용기 내부물을 흔들어 노심 냉각 능력을 크게 해치지 않게 한다. 근거는 경계 용어를 liquid-metal reactor%%t:LMR%% 업계 용어로 바꾸고 가정 반응도 사고 목록을 지웠다고 적는다. 기준 29는 GDC%%t:GDC%%와 같으며, 보호 계통과 반응도 제어 계통이 AOO%%t:AOO%%에서 안전 기능을 수행할 확률이 극히 높도록 설계하게 한다.
