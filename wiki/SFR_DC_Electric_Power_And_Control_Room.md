# SFR-DC 전력 계통과 제어실 (SFR-DC Electric Power and Control Room, Criteria 17–19)

> [!abstract] 요약
> 기준 17–19는 전력 계통, 전력 계통의 검사·시험, 제어실을 다룬다. 전력 계통 기준은 소외 전원 언급을 없애고 전체 전원 공급 방식의 신뢰성에 초점을 두며, 제어실 기준은 advanced reactor design criteria에 더해 사고 조건의 소듐 에어로졸 방호를 요구한다.

## 원문 사실
- "Electric power systems shall be provided when required to permit functioning of structures, systems, and components. The safety function for each power system shall be to provide sufficient capacity and capability to ensure that (1) that the design limits for the fission product barriers are not exceeded as a result of anticipated operational occurrences and (2) safety functions that rely on electric power are maintained in the event of postulated accidents." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 17 p.68]
- "The electric power systems shall include an onsite power system and an additional power system. The onsite electric power system shall have sufficient independence, redundancy, and testability to perform its safety functions, assuming a single failure. An additional power system shall have sufficient independence and testability to perform its safety function." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 17 p.69]
- "If electric power is not needed for anticipated operational occurrences or postulated accidents, the design shall demonstrate that power for important to safety functions is provided." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 17 p.69]
- "The electric power systems are required to provide reliable power for SSCs during anticipated operational occurrences or postulated accident conditions when those SSCs’ safety functions require electric power. The safety functions are established by the safety analyses (i.e. design basis accidents). Where electric power is needed for anticipated operational occurrences or postulated accidents, the electric power systems shall be sufficient in capacity and capability to ensure that safety functions as well as important to safety functions are maintained. The electric power systems provide redundancy and defense-in-depth since there would be a minimum of two power systems." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 17 p.68]
- "Compared to GDC 17, more emphasis is placed herein on requiring reliability of the overall power supply scheme rather than fully prescribing how such reliability can be attained. For example, reference to offsite electric power systems was deleted to provide for those reactor designs that do not depend on offsite power for the functioning of SSCs important to safety or do not connect to a power grid." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 17 p.69]
- "The onsite power system is envisioned as a fully Class 1E power system and the additional power system is left to the discretion of the designer as long as it meets the performance criteria in paragraph one and the design criteria of paragraph two. For example, the additional independent power source could be from the electrical grid, a diesel generator, a combustion gas turbine or some other alternative, again, at the discretion of the designer." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 17 p.69]
- "In this context, important to safety functions refer to the broader, potentially non-safety related functions such as post-accident monitoring, control room habitability, emergency lighting, radiation monitoring, communications and/or any others that may be deemed appropriate for the given design. The electric power system for important to safety functions could be non-Class 1E and would not be required to have redundant power sources." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 17 p.69]
- "Electric power systems important to safety shall be designed to permit appropriate periodic inspection and testing of important areas and features, such as wiring, insulation, connections, and switchboards, to assess the continuity of the systems and the condition of their components. The systems shall be designed with a capability to test periodically (1) the operability and functional performance of the components of the systems, such as onsite power sources, relays, switches, and buses, and (2) the operability of the systems as a whole and, under conditions as close to design as practical, the full operation sequence that brings the systems into operation, including operation of applicable portions of the protection system, and the transfer of power among systems." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 18 p.70]
- "ARDC 18 is a design-independent companion criterion to ARDC 17." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 18 p.69]
- "Wording pertaining to additional system examples has been deleted to allow increased flexibility associated with various designs. Specifically, the text related to the nuclear power unit, offsite power system, and onsite power system was deleted to be consistent with ARDC 17." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 18 p.70]
- "A control room shall be provided from which actions can be taken to operate the nuclear power unit safely under normal conditions and to maintain it in a safe condition under accident conditions. Adequate radiation protection shall be provided to permit access and occupancy of the control room under accident conditions without personnel receiving radiation exposures in excess of 5 rem total effective dose equivalent, as defined in § 50.2 for the duration of the accident." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 19 p.70]
- "Adequate habitability measures shall be provided to permit access and occupancy of the control room during normal operations and under accident conditions." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 19 p.70]
- "Adequate protection against sodium aerosols shall be provided to permit access and occupancy of the control room under accident conditions." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 19 p.70]
- "Equipment at appropriate locations outside the control room shall be provided (1) with a design capability for prompt hot shutdown of the reactor, including necessary instrumentation and controls to maintain the unit in a safe condition during hot shutdown, and (2) with a potential capability for subsequent cold shutdown of the reactor through the use of suitable procedures." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 19 p.71]
- "ARDC 19 preserves the language of GDC 19 which states (with emphasis added) “A control room shall be provided from which actions can be taken to operate the nuclear power unit safely…” However some clarification of this language is warranted." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 19 p.70]
- "It is clear from this language that there is a need to for operators to be able to take “actions” to control the plant. Therefore, designers must consider how the design of controls support safe operator actions. In addition, NRC staff recognize that in order for operators to act “safely” as stated in ARDC, that operators must have certain knowledge about the status of the plant and be able to make decisions about the appropriate course of action given a particular operating circumstance. Therefore, these cognitive needs of operators should also be considered by designers when interpreting ARDC 19." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 19 p.70]
- "This consideration should be reflected in the design of indications, displays, alarms, controls or other future technologies which are used to inform operators of plant status and may be used to support the decision making process (such as computer based procedures)." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 19 p.70]
- "This position is consistent with 10 CFR 50.34(f)(2)(iii) which describes the contents required in applications for construction permits. Amongst many other requirements, this rule indicates that the control room design must reflect “state-of-the-art human factors principles.” These state-of-the-art principles inherently consider both the cognitive and physical aspects of operator action as described above." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 19 p.71]
- "The criterion was updated to remove specific emphasis on LOCAs, which may be not appropriate for advanced designs." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 19 p.71]
- "Reference to “whole body, or its equivalent to any part of the body” has been updated to the current total effective dose equivalent standard as defined in § 50.2." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 19 p.71]
- "An additional control room habitability requirement has been proposed. It addresses a new concern: accidents that are not radiological in nature may also affect control room access and occupancy. This may include accidental sodium leakage and sodium fire, which could release sodium aerosols." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 19 p.71]
- "The last paragraph of the GDC has been eliminated for the SFR-DC because it is not applicable to future applicants." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 19 p.71]
- "A control room shall be provided from which actions can be taken to operate the nuclear power unit safely under normal conditions and to maintain it in a safe condition under accident conditions. Adequate radiation protection shall be provided to permit access and occupancy of the control room under accident conditions without personnel receiving radiation exposures in excess of 5 rem total effective dose equivalent as defined in § 50.2 for the duration of the accident." [RG_1.232 ARDC 19 p.35]
- "Adequate habitability measures shall be provided to permit access and occupancy of the control room during normal operations and under accident conditions. Equipment at appropriate locations outside the control room shall be provided (1) with a design capability for prompt hot shutdown of the reactor, including necessary instrumentation and controls to maintain the unit in a safe condition during hot shutdown, and (2) with a potential capability for subsequent cold shutdown of the reactor through the use of suitable procedures." [RG_1.232 ARDC 19 p.35]

## 해설
기준 17은 advanced reactor design criteria와 같다. 구조물·계통·기기의 기능에 전력이 필요할 때 전력 계통을 두고, 각 전력 계통이 Anticipated Operational Occurrence%%t:AOO%%로 핵분열생성물 barrier%%t:BARRIER%%의 설계 한계를 넘지 않게 하고 가정 사고에서 전력에 의존하는 안전 기능을 유지할 용량과 능력을 갖게 한다. 전력 계통은 소내 전력 계통과 추가 전력 계통으로 이루어지며, 소내 계통은 단일 고장을 가정해도 안전 기능을 수행할 독립성·중복성·시험 가능성을, 추가 계통은 독립성과 시험 가능성을 가져야 한다. AOO%%t:AOO%%나 가정 사고에 전력이 필요하지 않은 설계도 important to safety 기능에 전력이 공급됨을 입증해야 한다. 근거는 general design criteria%%t:GDC%%보다 전체 전원 공급 방식의 신뢰성 요구를 강조하고, 소외 전원에 의존하지 않거나 전력망에 연결하지 않는 설계를 고려해 소외 전력 계통 언급을 삭제했다고 설명한다. 소내 계통은 완전한 Class 1E 전력 계통으로 상정하고, 추가 계통은 성능·설계 기준을 만족하는 한 전력망, 디젤 발전기, 연소 가스 터빈 같은 대안 가운데 설계자가 정할 수 있다. 여기서 important to safety 기능은 사고 후 감시, 제어실 거주성, 비상 조명, 방사선 감시, 통신 같은 넓은 기능을 뜻하며, 그 전력 계통은 non-Class 1E일 수 있고 중복 전원이 요구되지 않는다.

기준 18도 advanced reactor design criteria와 같다. important to safety 전력 계통을 배선, 절연, 연결부, 배전반 같은 중요 부위의 주기적 검사·시험이 가능하도록 설계하고, 소내 전원, 계전기, 스위치, 모선 같은 구성품의 운전성과 기능 성능, 그리고 보호 계통의 해당 부분과 계통 간 전력 전환을 포함한 계통 전체의 운전 순서를 설계 조건에 가깝게 주기적으로 시험할 수 있게 한다. 근거는 이 기준이 기준 17과 짝을 이루는 설계 독립적 기준이며, 호기, 소외 전력 계통, 소내 전력 계통에 관한 문구를 기준 17과 일관되게 삭제했다고 적는다.

기준 19는 수정된 기준이다. 정상 조건에서 호기를 안전하게 운전하고 사고 조건에서 안전 상태를 유지하는 조치를 취할 수 있는 제어실을 두고, 사고 기간 동안 종사자가 § 50.2에 정의된 total effective dose equivalent 5 rem을 넘지 않고 제어실에 접근·거주할 수 있는 방사선 방호와, 정상 운전과 사고 조건의 거주성 조치를 요구한다. advanced reactor design criteria의 같은 번호 기준에 더해, 사고 조건에서 제어실 접근·거주를 위한 소듐 에어로졸 방호를 요구한다. 제어실 밖 적절한 위치에는 신속한 고온 정지 능력과 이후 저온 정지의 잠재 능력을 갖춘 설비를 둔다. 근거는 운전원이 발전소를 제어할 조치를 취하고 안전하게 행동하려면 발전소 상태를 알고 결정을 내릴 수 있어야 하므로 설계자가 제어 설계와 운전원의 인지적 요구를 함께 고려해야 하며, 이것이 건설허가 신청서의 제어실 설계에 최신 인간공학 원칙을 요구하는 10 CFR 50.34(f)(2)(iii)와 일관된다고 설명한다. 근거는 또 신형 설계에 맞지 않을 수 있는 loss of coolant accident%%t:LOCA%% 강조를 없애고 선량 기준을 § 50.2의 total effective dose equivalent로 갱신했으며, 소듐 누설과 소듐 화재가 내는 소듐 에어로졸처럼 방사선학적이지 않은 사고도 제어실 접근·거주에 영향을 줄 수 있어 거주성 요건을 추가했고, GDC%%t:GDC%%의 마지막 문단은 장래 신청자에게 해당하지 않아 삭제했다고 적는다.
