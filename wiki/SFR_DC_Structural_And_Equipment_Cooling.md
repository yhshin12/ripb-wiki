# SFR-DC 구조물·기기 냉각 (SFR-DC Structural and Equipment Cooling, Criteria 44–46)

> [!abstract] 요약
> 기준 44–46은 general design criteria%%t:GDC%%의 cooling water 기준을 structural and equipment cooling으로 바꾼 기준으로, important to safety 구조물·계통·기기의 열을 최종 열침원으로 전달하는 계통과 그 검사·시험을 다룬다. 세 기준은 advanced reactor design criteria와 같다.

## 원문 사실
- "A system to transfer heat from structures, systems, and components important to safety to an ultimate heat sink shall be provided, as necessary, to transfer the combined heat load of these structures, systems, and components under normal operating and accident conditions." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 44 p.88]
- "Suitable redundancy in components and features and suitable interconnections, leak detection, and isolation capabilities shall be provided to ensure that the system safety function can be accomplished, assuming a single failure." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 44 p.88]
- "This renamed SFR-DC accounts for advanced reactor system design differences to include cooling requirements for SSCs, if applicable; this SFR-DC does not address the residual heat removal system required under SFR-DC 34, and ECCS system under SFR-DC 35." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 44 p.88]
- "The GDC reference to electric power was removed. Refer to SFR-DC17 concerning those systems that require electric power." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 44 p.88]
- "A system to transfer heat from structures, systems, and components important to safety to an ultimate heat sink shall be provided, as necessary, to transfer the combined heat load of these structures, systems, and components under normal operating and accident conditions." [RG_1.232 ARDC 44 p.52]
- "Suitable redundancy in components and features and suitable interconnections, leak detection, and isolation capabilities shall be provided to ensure that the system safety function can be accomplished, assuming a single failure." [RG_1.232 ARDC 44 p.52]

- "This renamed ARDC accounts for advanced reactor system design differences to include possible cooling requirements for SSCs important to safety." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 45 p.88]
- "The structural and equipment cooling systems shall be designed to permit appropriate periodic inspection of important components, such as heat exchangers and piping, to ensure the integrity and capability of the systems." [RG_1.232 ARDC 45 p.52]
- "The structural and equipment cooling systems shall be designed to permit appropriate periodic functional testing to ensure (1) the structural and leaktight integrity of their components, (2) the operability and performance of the system components, and (3) the operability of the systems as a whole and, under conditions as close to design as practical, the performance of the full operational sequences that bring the systems into operation for reactor shutdown and postulated accidents, including the operation of associated systems." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 46 p.88]
- "This renamed ARDC accounts for advanced reactor system design differences to include possible cooling requirements for SSCs important to safety. Specific mention of “pressure” testing has been removed yet remains a potential requirement should it be necessary as a component of “…appropriate periodic functional testing...” of cooling systems. The LOCA reference has been removed to provide for any postulated accident that might affect subject SSCs. The GDC reference to electric power was removed. ## 해설
기준 44는 important to safety Structures, Systems, and Components%%t:SSC%%의 열을 최종 열침원으로 전달하는 계통을 필요한 경우 두어 정상 운전과 사고 조건의 결합 열부하를 전달하게 하고, 단일 고장을 가정해도 안전 기능을 수행하도록 기기·특성의 중복성과 연결, 누설 탐지, 격리 능력을 요구한다. 근거는 이름을 바꾼 이 기준이 신형로 계통 설계의 차이를 반영해 해당하는 경우 SSC%%t:SSC%% 냉각 요건을 포함하도록 했으며, 기준 34의 잔열 제거 계통과 기준 35의 emergency core cooling system%%t:ECCS%%은 다루지 않는다고 적는다. 전력 언급은 전력 계통 기준을 참조하도록 삭제했다.

기준 45는 열교환기와 배관 같은 중요 기기의 주기적 검사가 가능하도록 구조물·기기 냉각 계통을 설계하게 한다. 기준 46은 기기의 구조적·누설 건전성, 기기의 운전성과 성능, 그리고 관련 계통 운전을 포함해 원자로 정지와 가정 사고에서 계통을 기동하는 전체 운전 순서를 설계 조건에 가깝게 확인하는 주기적 기능 시험이 가능하도록 설계하게 한다. 근거는 압력 시험 언급을 뺐지만 필요하면 여전히 요구될 수 있고, 누설이 모든 조건에서 안전 기능과 심층방어(Defense-in-Depth)%%t:DID%%에 영향을 주지 않으면 누설 밀폐가 아닌 계통도 일부 설계에서 수용될 수 있으며, 능동·피동과 무관하게 기기 시험이 필요해 Active를 지웠고, 영향을 받을 수 있는 모든 가정 사고를 다루려고 loss of coolant accident%%t:LOCA%% 언급을 없앴다고 설명한다.
