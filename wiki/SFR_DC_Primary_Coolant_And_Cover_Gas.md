# SFR-DC primary coolant·cover gas와 계통 접속 (SFR-DC Primary Coolant, Cover Gas, and Interfaces, Criteria 71, 78, 79)

> [!abstract] 요약
> 기준 71, 78, 79는 general design criteria%%t:GDC%%에 대응 조문이 없는 추가 기준으로, 1차 냉각재 소듐과 커버가스(cover gas)%%t:COVER_GAS%%의 순도 제어, 1차 냉각 계통과 다른 계통의 접속부 barrier%%t:BARRIER%%, 커버가스%%t:COVER_GAS%% 재고 유지를 다룬다. 기준 78은 화학적으로 양립하지 않는 유체와의 접속부에 중복된 두 피동 barrier%%t:BARRIER%%를 요구하고, 양립하는 유체에는 조건부로 단일 피동 barrier%%t:BARRIER%%를 허용한다.

## 원문 사실
- "Systems shall be provided as necessary to maintain the purity of primary coolant sodium and cover gas within specified design limits. These limits shall be based on consideration of (1) chemical attack, (2) fouling and plugging of passages, and (3) radionuclide concentrations, and (4) air or moisture ingress as a result of a leak of cover gas." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 71 p.97]
- "The NRC considered DOE’s proposed SFR-DC 71 and made changes based on the “Response to NRC Staff Questions on the U.S. Department of Energy Report, ‘Guidance for Developing Principal Design Criteria for Advanced Non-Light Water Reactors’” (pages 12-13) (Ref. 19)." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 71 p.97]
- "NUREG 1368 (Ref. 4) (page 3-57), Section 3.2.4.6, suggested the need for a separate criterion for a sodium and cover gas purity control. Also a separate criterion was included in NUREG-0968 (Ref. 5) (Criterion 34, “Reactor and Intermediate Coolant and Cover Gas Purity Control”)." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 71 p.97]
- "When the primary coolant system interfaces with a structure, system, or component containing fluid that is chemically incompatible with the primary coolant, the interface location shall be designed to ensure that the primary coolant is separated from the chemically incompatible fluid by two redundant, passive barriers. When the primary coolant system interfaces with a structure, system, or component containing fluid that is chemically compatible with the primary coolant, then the interface location may be a single passive barrier provided that the following conditions are met:" [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 78 p.100]
- "(1) postulated leakage at the interface location does not result in failure of the intended safety functions of structures, systems or components important to safety or result in exceeding the fuel design limits" [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 78 p.101]
- "(2) the fluid contained in the structure, system, or component is maintained at a higher pressure than the primary coolant during normal operation, anticipated operational occurrences, shutdown, and accident conditions." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 78 p.101]
- "The consequence of leakage between the primary coolant system and a heat removal system (i.e. residual heat removal system, intermediate coolant system) is more significant for primary coolant system (potentially impacting the fuel design limits or integrity of the primary coolant boundary) than it is for the heat removal system (coolant drawdown or introduction of radioactive sodium)." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 78 p.100]
- "Rather than creating two parallel requirements for the two systems, SFR-DC 78 was created to discuss leakage and required barriers as a generic criterion. The criterion allows for double walled steam generators, intermediate coolant systems connected to steam power system, and systems similar to the PRISM Direct Reactor Auxiliary Cooling System (DRACS)." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 78 p.100]
- "A paragraph from NUREG 1368 (page 3-41) (Ref. 4) was added describing the characteristics of the residual heat removal working fluid and its associated operating pressure. This SFR-DC has been worded to explain that an intermediate coolant system may be used if the primary coolant is not chemically compatible with the energy conversion system coolant." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 78 p.101]
- "A single passive barrier is adequate defense in depth when the heat removal working fluid is chemically compatible with the primary coolant, such that postulated leakage between the two systems does not result in the failure of any intended safety function of any SSC important to safety or cause fuel design limits to be exceeded." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 78 p.101]
- "An example is a heat removal system with liquid sodium potassium (NaK). A liquid sodium primary coolant system that is contaminated with NaK may have phase changes (e.g., solidification, boiling) at different temperatures, without adversely affecting the overall system. The postulated leakage may be based upon a leak-before-break analysis or the ability to detect leakage between the primary and intermediate coolant systems. If the working fluids are not chemically compatible, at least two passive barriers must separate the two systems." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 78 p.101]
- "The higher pressure requirement is to ensure any leakage in the interface between the two systems does not result in a release of radioactive primary coolant to the nonradioactive part of the heat transport system." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 78 p.101]
- "A sentence has been added to explain that this differential pressure requirement must be satisfied during AOOs and design-basis accidents, as well as during normal operating and shutdown conditions." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 78 p.101]
- "A system to maintain cover gas inventory shall be provided as necessary to ensure that the primary coolant sodium design limits are not exceeded as a result of cover gas loss due to leakage from the primary coolant boundary and rupture of small piping or other small components that are part of the primary coolant boundary." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 79 p.102]
- "This criterion is similar to GDC 33 in 10 CFR Part 50, Appendix A and SFR-DC 33 in this document. GDC 33 and SFR-DC 33 focus on the effects of primary coolant (sodium) loss. A leak in a SFR primary coolant system may expel the cover gas rather than the primary coolant. The cover gas in the SFR performs an important to safety function by protecting the sodium coolant from chemical reactions. The staff created a new SFR-DC rather than adding the cover gas in the term “primary coolant.” The term “primary coolant sodium design limits” is used to maintain consistent terminology with SFR-DC 71. The primary coolant sodium design limits consider the possibility of interactions between the primary coolant sodium and the primary coolant boundary or the fuel due to changes in the chemistry of the primary coolant sodium. The considerations include the possibility of (1) chemical attack, (2) fouling and plugging of passages, (3) radionuclide concentrations, and (4) air or moisture ingress as a result of a leak of cover gas." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 79 p.102]
- "The term “as necessary” is retained from SFR-DC 33 to permit designer flexibility if leakage of the system does not challenge the design limits of the primary coolant (for instance, an inerted containment filled with Argon)." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 79 p.102]
- "“Reactor coolant pressure boundary” has been relabeled as “primary coolant boundary” to conform to standard terms used in the LMR industry." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 79 p.102]
- "The use of the term “primary” indicates that the SFR-DC are applicable only to the primary cooling system, not the intermediate cooling system." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 79 p.102]
- "The cover gas boundary is included as part of the primary coolant boundary (referred to as RCPB by PRISM) per NUREG-1368 (page 3-38) (Ref. 4)." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 79 p.103]
- "The GDC reference to electric power was removed. Refer to SFR-DC 17 concerning those systems that require electric power." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 79 p.103]

## 해설
기준 71은 필요한 경우 1차 냉각재 소듐과 커버가스%%t:COVER_GAS%%의 순도를 지정된 설계 한계 안에 유지하는 계통을 두게 하며, 그 한계는 화학적 공격, 유로의 오염과 막힘, 방사성 핵종 농도, 커버가스%%t:COVER_GAS%% 누설에 따른 공기나 수분 유입의 네 가지를 고려해 정한다. 근거는 NRC가 U.S. Department of Energy%%t:DOE%%의 제안 기준을 검토하고 NRC 직원 질의에 대한 응답 문서를 바탕으로 고쳤다고 적는다. 또 `NUREG-1368`은 별도 기준이 필요하다고 제시했고 `NUREG-0968`에는 별도 기준이 있었다.

기준 78은 1차 냉각 계통이 1차 냉각재와 화학적으로 양립하지 않는 유체를 담은 구조물·계통·기기와 접속하면 접속부에서 두 유체를 중복된 두 피동 barrier%%t:BARRIER%%로 분리하게 한다. 양립하는 유체와 접속하면 두 조건을 만족할 때 단일 피동 barrier%%t:BARRIER%%를 허용한다. 첫째, 접속부에서 가정한 누설이 important to safety Structures, Systems, and Components%%t:SSC%%의 안전 기능 상실이나 핵연료 설계 한계 초과로 이어지지 않아야 한다. 둘째, 상대 계통의 유체가 정상 운전, Anticipated Operational Occurrence%%t:AOO%%, 정지, 사고 조건 동안 1차 냉각재보다 높은 압력으로 유지되어야 한다.

근거에 따르면 1차 냉각 계통과 잔열 제거 계통이나 intermediate coolant system 같은 열제거 계통 사이의 누설은 열제거 계통 쪽의 냉각재 감소나 방사성 소듐 유입보다 1차 냉각 계통 쪽에서 핵연료 설계 한계나 primary coolant boundary 건전성에 영향을 줄 수 있어 결말이 더 크다. 직원은 두 계통에 평행한 요건을 따로 두는 대신 누설과 필요한 barrier%%t:BARRIER%%를 다루는 일반 기준으로 기준 78을 만들었고, 이 기준은 이중벽 증기 발생기, 증기 동력 계통에 연결된 intermediate coolant system, direct reactor auxiliary cooling system%%t:DRACS%%과 비슷한 계통을 허용한다. 또 1차 냉각재가 에너지 변환 계통 냉각재와 화학적으로 양립하지 않으면 intermediate coolant system을 쓸 수 있음을 설명하도록 표현했다. 열제거 작동 유체가 1차 냉각재와 화학적으로 양립해 가정한 누설이 안전 기능 상실이나 핵연료 설계 한계 초과를 일으키지 않으면 단일 피동 barrier%%t:BARRIER%%가 적절한 심층방어(Defense-in-Depth)%%t:DID%%이다. 근거가 드는 예는 액체 sodium potassium을 쓰는 열제거 계통으로, 이것이 섞인 액체 소듐 1차 냉각 계통은 다른 온도에서 상변화를 겪을 수 있지만 전체 계통에 악영향을 주지 않는다. 가정 누설은 leak-before-break 해석이나 1차·중간 냉각 계통 사이 누설 탐지 능력에 근거할 수 있고, 작동 유체가 양립하지 않으면 적어도 두 피동 barrier%%t:BARRIER%%가 두 계통을 분리해야 한다. 높은 압력 요건은 접속부 누설이 방사성 1차 냉각재를 열 수송 계통의 비방사성 부분으로 방출하지 않게 하려는 것이며, 이 차압 요건이 정상 운전과 정지뿐 아니라 AOO%%t:AOO%%와 design-basis accidents 동안에도 만족되어야 함을 설명하는 문장을 더했다.

기준 79는 primary coolant boundary에서의 누설이나 그 일부인 소형 배관·기기의 파열로 커버가스%%t:COVER_GAS%%를 잃어 1차 냉각재 소듐 설계 한계를 넘지 않도록, 필요한 경우 커버가스%%t:COVER_GAS%% 재고를 유지하는 계통을 두게 한다. 근거는 이 기준이 GDC%%t:GDC%% 33 및 기준 33과 비슷하지만 그 두 조문은 1차 냉각재 상실의 영향에 초점을 두며, sodium-cooled fast reactor%%t:SFR%% 1차 냉각 계통의 누설은 냉각재보다 커버가스%%t:COVER_GAS%%를 내보낼 수 있다고 설명한다. 커버가스%%t:COVER_GAS%%는 소듐 냉각재를 화학 반응에서 보호하는 important to safety 기능을 하며, 직원은 primary coolant라는 말에 커버가스%%t:COVER_GAS%%를 넣는 대신 새 기준을 만들었다. primary coolant sodium design limits라는 용어는 기준 71과 일관되게 쓰였고, 1차 냉각재 소듐의 화학 변화로 인한 소듐과 primary coolant boundary나 핵연료 사이의 상호작용을 기준 71과 같은 네 가지 측면에서 고려한다. as necessary라는 말은 계통 누설이 1차 냉각재 설계 한계를 위협하지 않는 경우, 예컨대 아르곤으로 채운 불활성 격납의 경우 설계자의 유연성을 주려고 기준 33에서 가져왔다. 근거는 또 reactor coolant pressure boundary를 liquid-metal reactor%%t:LMR%% 업계 표준 용어에 맞춰 primary coolant boundary로 바꾸고 커버가스%%t:COVER_GAS%% 경계를 그 일부로 두었으며 전력 언급을 지웠다고 적는다. 기준 79는 표 1에서 GDC%%t:GDC%% 제목이 N/A인 추가 기준이다.
