# RG 1.233의 NEI 18-04 승인 범위 (NRC Endorsement of NEI 18-04 in RG 1.233)

> [!abstract] 요약
> regulatory guide%%t:RG%% 1.233은 NEI 18-04 Revision 1을 비경수형원전(non-light-water reactor)%%t:NON_LWR%%의 Licensing Basis Event%%t:LBE%% 선정, Structures, Systems, and Components%%t:SSC%% 분류와 special treatment%%t:SPECIAL_TREATMENT%%, 심층방어(Defense-in-Depth)%%t:DID%% 평가를 위한 하나의 수용 가능한 방법으로 승인한다. NRC 직원은 NEI 18-04에 중대한 예외를 두지 않고 명확화와 강조점을 제시하며, 핵심 차이 하나로 NEI 18-04의 safety related 정의가 10 CFR 50.2의 정의와 다르다는 점을 든다.

## 원문 사실
- "This regulatory guide (RG) provides the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) staff’s guidance on using a technology-inclusive, risk-informed, and performance-based methodology to inform the licensing basis and content of applications for non-light-water reactors (non-LWRs), including, but not limited to, molten salt reactors, high-temperature gas-cooled reactors, and a variety of fast reactors at different thermal capacities." [RG_1.233 §A Purpose p.1]
- "The selection of licensing-basis events (LBEs); classification and special treatments of structures, systems, and components (SSCs); and assessment of defense in depth (DID) are fundamental to the safe design of non-LWRs." [RG_1.233 §A Purpose p.1]
- "as one acceptable method for non-LWR designers to use when carrying out these activities and preparing their applications." [RG_1.233 §A Purpose p.1]
- "Although the technology-inclusive methodology provides a common approach to selecting LBEs, classifying SSCs, and assessing DID, the applicability of specific technical requirements in NRC regulations or the need to define additional technical requirements arising from the safety evaluations is made on a case-by-case basis for each non-LWR design." [RG_1.233 §A Purpose p.1]
- "This RG applies to nuclear power reactor designers, applicants, and licensees of advanced non-LWR designs applying for permits, licenses, certifications, and approvals under 10 CFR Part 50 and 10 CFR Part 52." [RG_1.233 §A Applicability p.2]
- "Regulatory guides are not substitutes for regulations and compliance with them is not required." [RG_1.233 §A Purpose of Regulatory Guides p.4]
- "The staff takes no significant exceptions to the guidance in NEI 18-04 but does provide clarifications and points of emphasis as detailed in this RG." [RG_1.233 §B Background p.6]
- "The variety of non-LWR technologies, which use different coolants, fuel forms, and safety system designs, make it necessary to define a methodology as opposed to developing prescriptive guidance on the content of applications, such as that prepared for light-water reactors (LWRs)." [RG_1.233 §B Background p.6]
- "The methodology’s focus on those measures needed to address risks posed by non-LWR technologies will help an applicant provide sufficient information on the design and programmatic controls, while avoiding an excessive level of detail on less important parts of a plant." [RG_1.233 §B Background p.6]
- "The LMP activities led to the publication and submittal of NEI 18-04." [RG_1.233 §B Background p.8]
- "The guidance in this RG for licensing non-LWR technologies will need to be supplemented by other RGs and documents to help non-LWR developers and the NRC staff prepare and review applications for licenses, certifications, and approvals." [RG_1.233 §B Intended Use p.9]
- "This RG and the NEI technical document are, with the exception of technology-specific topics, generally consistent with the principles and guidance in the IAEA document series, including the IAEA documents listed below." [RG_1.233 §B Harmonization with International Standards p.9]
- "If the secondary reference has neither been incorporated by reference into NRC regulations nor endorsed in a RG, then the secondary reference is neither a legally-binding requirement nor a “generic” NRC approved acceptable approach for meeting an NRC requirement." [RG_1.233 §B Documents Discussed in Staff Regulatory Guidance p.9]
- "One key distinction between NEI 18-04 and current NRC guidance and regulations developed for LWRs is that the definition of “safety related” SSCs in NEI 18-04 is different from the NRC’s definition of “safety-related” SSCs in 10 CFR 50.2." [RG_1.233 §C p.11]
- "The NRC staff has determined that the methods described in NEI 18-04 constitute one acceptable means to identify LBEs, classify SSCs, establish special treatments, identify programmatic controls, and assess DID for non-LWRs." [RG_1.233 §C p.11]
- "Each section in NEI 18-04 summarized below is part of an integrated methodology that includes defined relationships among LBEs, equipment classification, special treatments, programmatic controls, and assessments of DID." [RG_1.233 §C p.11]
- "The applicants perform the evaluations in an iterative fashion as they develop the design and licensing strategies." [RG_1.233 §C p.11]
- "Section 5.9.6 in NEI 18-04 discusses change control processes following the issuance of a license, certification, or approval. The staff makes no findings on this topic." [RG_1.233 §C.3.a p.22]
- "However, the NRC staff does not intend to use the guidance in this regulatory guide to support NRC staff actions in a manner that would constitute backfitting as that term is defined in 10 CFR 50.109" [RG_1.233 §D p.27]
- "nor does the NRC staff intend to use the guidance to affect the issue finality of an approval under 10 CFR Part 52" [RG_1.233 §D p.27]
- "Nuclear Energy Institute (NEI) 18-04, “Risk-Informed Performance-Based Technology-Inclusive Guidance for Non-Light Water Reactor Licensing Basis Development,” Revision 1, August 2019." [RG_1.233 §REFERENCES p.29]

## 해설
이 지침은 molten salt reactor%%t:MSR%%, high-temperature gas-cooled reactors, 다양한 열출력의 fast reactors를 포함한 비경수형원전%%t:NON_LWR%%의 인허가 기준과 신청서 내용을 technology-inclusive, risk-informed, performance-based 방법론으로 정하는 NRC 직원 지침이며, 10 CFR Part 50과 Part 52에 따라 허가·인증·승인을 신청하는 신형 비경수형원전%%t:NON_LWR%% 설계자, 신청자, 인허가 소지자에게 적용된다. 원문은 LBE%%t:LBE%% 선정, SSC%%t:SSC%% 분류와 special treatment%%t:SPECIAL_TREATMENT%%, 심층방어%%t:DID%% 평가가 비경수형원전%%t:NON_LWR%%의 안전한 설계에 기본이라고 적는다. 방법론은 이 활동들에 공통 접근을 제공하지만, NRC 규정의 특정 기술 요건이 적용되는지나 안전 평가에서 추가 기술 요건이 필요한지는 설계마다 사안별로 판단한다. 규제 지침은 규정을 대체하지 않으며 준수가 요구되지 않는다.

NRC 직원은 NEI 18-04에 중대한 예외를 두지 않고 명확화와 강조점을 제시한다. 원문은 냉각재, 핵연료 형태, 안전 계통 설계가 서로 다른 기술들이 있어 LWR처럼 신청서 내용에 대한 처방적 지침을 만들기보다 방법론을 정의할 필요가 있다고 설명하고, 이 방법론이 비경수형원전%%t:NON_LWR%% 기술의 위험을 다루는 조치에 초점을 두어 덜 중요한 부분의 과도한 상세를 피하면서 충분한 정보를 제공하게 한다고 적는다. 직원은 NEI 18-04의 방법이 LBE%%t:LBE%% 식별, SSC%%t:SSC%% 분류, special treatment%%t:SPECIAL_TREATMENT%% 설정, programmatic controls 식별, 심층방어%%t:DID%% 평가의 하나의 수용 가능한 수단이라고 판단했다. NEI 18-04의 각 절은 LBE%%t:LBE%%, 기기 분류, special treatment%%t:SPECIAL_TREATMENT%%, programmatic controls, 심층방어%%t:DID%% 평가 사이에 정해진 관계가 있는 통합 방법론의 일부이며, 신청자는 설계와 인허가 전략을 발전시키면서 평가를 반복한다.

핵심 차이 하나는 NEI 18-04의 safety related SSC%%t:SSC%% 정의가 10 CFR 50.2의 safety-related 정의와 다르다는 점이다. 직원은 인허가 발급 뒤의 변경 관리 과정에 관한 NEI 18-04의 논의에 대해서는 판단하지 않는다고 적는다. 지침 본문은 NEI 18-04 제목을 Technology-Inclusive 없이 적고, 참고문헌은 Technology-Inclusive를 넣어 적는다.

지침은 다른 RG%%t:RG%%와 문서로 보완되어야 한다. 지침과 NEI 문서는 기술 고유 주제를 빼면 IAEA 문서 체계의 원칙과 지침에 대체로 부합한다. 규정에 참조 포함되지도 RG%%t:RG%%에서 승인되지도 않은 secondary reference는 법적 구속력 있는 요건도 일반적으로 승인된 방법도 아니다. 직원은 이 지침을 10 CFR 50.109의 backfitting에 해당하는 방식이나 10 CFR Part 52 승인의 issue finality에 영향을 주는 방식으로 쓰지 않겠다고 적는다. Licensing Modernization Project%%t:LMP%% 활동의 결과로 NEI 18-04가 발간·제출되었다.
