# NRC의 사건군별 선원항 평가 (NRC Evaluation of Event-Group Source Terms)

> [!abstract] 요약
> NRC staff는 `PSAR` Table 3.2-1~3.2-18의 선원항(source term)%%t:SOURCE_TERM%%을 네 사건군(용기 내 출력운전, 용기 내 연료취급, 용기 외 연료취급, 용기 외 계통 방출)으로 나누어 검토하고, 각 군의 선원항%%t:SOURCE_TERM%%이 사건을 합리적으로 대표한다고 판단했다. 다만 `SCG` 경로 방출과 `SPS` 셀 누설률 가정은 operating license%%t:OL%% 단계에서 다시 평가하도록 남겼고, `SPS` 셀 누설률은 비보수적일 수 있다고 적었다.

## 원문 사실
- "These MSTs are representative of LBEs and DBAs with an intact vessel. These events include breach of the fuel cladding, but do not result in fuel melting." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.1 p.193]
- "The local fault events result in cladding damage to one fuel assembly, while the MST for the protected loss of flow (PLOF) or loss of offsite power (LOOP) events is based on damage to the cladding in 2/3 of the core." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.1 p.193]
- "Leakage across the intact reactor vessel head barrier from the cover gas space to the HAA is modeled as an assumed leakage rate of 1 percent of the cover gas volume per day." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.1 p.194]
- "Leakage from the intact HAA space was modeled directly to the environment (no credit for the RXB) as an assumed leakage rate of 10 percent of the HAA volume per day." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.1 p.194]
- "The local fault DBA assumed the degraded HAA barrier leaks to the environment at an elevated rate of 100 percent of the HAA volume per day." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.1 p.194]
- "The staff observed in the audit that the MST analyses provided justification for the pool scrubbing model assumptions and found through its assessment of the referenced reports that the modeling of sodium pool scrubbing is appropriate for the Natrium PSAR." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.1 p.194]
- "As noted in the PSAR, the dose consequences for the local fault events, the PLOF BDBE, and the LOOP BDBE do not include radionuclide release from SCG release pathways. The consequence analyses for these events will evaluate release from SCG release pathways, including the mitigation from SCG isolation, at the OL stage." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.1 p.195]
- "The staff determined through review of the PSAR, and confirmed through the audit of supporting documentation, that the PSAR tables 3.2-1, 3.2-2, and 3.2-4 source terms were developed with conservatively biased radionuclide release and transport assumptions to reasonably represent at-power in-vessel events." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.1 p.195]

- "The source term for the in-vessel fuel drop BDBEs assumes the leakage from the RVH degraded primary barrier is 100 percent of the cover gas volume per day to an enveloping barrier in the RXB which is made up of a portion of the RXB volume." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.2 p.199]
- "The DBA and DBE in-vessel fuel drop accidents model the release directly from the vessel to the environment, without credit for the HAA or RXB." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.2 p.199]
- "The staff determined through review of the PSAR and confirmed through the audit of supporting documentation that the PSAR tables 3.2-3, 3.2-5, and 3.2-6 source terms were developed with conservatively biased radionuclide release and transport assumptions to reasonably represent in-vessel fuel handling events." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.2 p.199]
- "The source terms assumed damage to a high burnup assembly, with the inventory decayed to reflect the event occurring at 2.5, 210, or 310 days post-shutdown, as applicable to the event scenario." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.3 p.201]
- "For the PSAR section 3.8.5.3 fuel handling event in the SFP consequence analysis, the MST was doubled to account for the failure of two spent fuel assemblies." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.3 p.202]
- "In the PSAR MST analysis, the modeling of the SFP is informed by RG 1.183 Appendix B guidance on radionuclide retention in the SFP for LWR FHAs, with a reduced DF to address the sodium bond-water reaction." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.3 p.202]
- "The staff’s judgment is that the modeling of radionuclide retention in the SFP is reasonable for the PSAR taking into consideration the applicant’s continuing investigation of the phenomena." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.3 p.203]
- "The staff determined through review of the PSAR and confirmed through the audit of supporting documentation that the PSAR tables 3.2-7 through 3.2-9 source terms are developed with conservatively biased radionuclide release and transport assumptions to reasonably represent ex-vessel fuel handling events." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.3 p.203]
- "These events are the DBE in PSAR section 3.7.4.4, BDBE in PSAR section 3.8.5.3, and the fuel handling accident (FHA) in the SFP DBA in PSAR section 3.9.5.2." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.3 p.202]

- "During the audit, USO identified that the PIC design has changed since the PSAR submittal and that the analysis of the ESWR events in PSAR are based on the outdated preliminary design information." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.3 p.203]
- "the staff determined that the application of the fuel handling event source terms to the ESWR events is reasonable for the PSAR." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.3 p.203]
- "The MST is modeled with the assumption that a small break in the SPS-P results in an airborne release equivalent to 20 gallons of aerosolized primary sodium to the SPS cell in the RAB." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.4 p.206]
- "The SPS cells leaks to the RAB at a leakage rate of 50 percent of the SPS cell volume per day, and the RAB exhausts to the environment instantaneously." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.4 p.206]
- "The staff determined through review of the PSAR and confirmed through the audit of supporting documentation that the PSAR tables 3.2-10, 3.2-11, and 3.2-18 source terms reasonably represent SPS release events." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.4 p.207]
- "Because the dominant isotope for these source terms is sodium-24, which has a half-life of approximately 15 hours, the leak rate assumptions of 10 percent and 50 percent have significant impacts on the source term results." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1.4 p.207]

## 해설
staff는 사건군마다 `PSAR` 선원항%%t:SOURCE_TERM%% 표가 사건을 합리적으로 대표한다고 결론지었다. 용기 내 사건, 용기 외 연료취급, `SCG`, `RWG` 사건은 보수적으로 치우친 방출·이동 가정으로 만들어졌다고 적었지만, `SPS` 사건에는 그 표현 대신 셀 누설률 가정이 비보수적일 수 있다는 단서를 달았다. 사건군별 요점은 다음과 같다.
- **용기 내 출력운전 사건:** 연료 피복 파손은 있으나 연료 용융은 없다. local fault 사건은 집합체 하나, `PLOF`와 `LOOP` 사건은 노심 2/3의 피복 손상을 기준으로 한다. 건전한 용기 헤드 barrier%%t:BARRIER%%는 하루 커버가스(cover gas)%%t:COVER_GAS%% 체적의 1 percent, 건전한 `HAA`는 10 percent로 누설한다고 모델링했다. local fault Design Basis Accident%%t:DBA%%는 열화된 `HAA`가 100 percent로 누설한다고 가정했다. pool scrubbing%%t:POOL_SCRUBBING%% 모델은 적절하다고 보았다. 이 사건들의 선량에는 `SCG` 경로 방출이 빠져 있어 OL%%t:OL%% 단계에서 `SCG` 격리를 포함해 평가한다.
- **용기 내 연료취급 사건:** 정지 후 0일 또는 2.5일의 고연소도 집합체 손상을 가정한다. Beyond Design Basis Event%%t:BDBE%%는 열화된 용기 헤드를 통해 하루 100 percent로 `RXB` 일부 체적에 방출한다고 보고, DBA%%t:DBA%%와 Design Basis Event%%t:DBE%%는 `HAA`나 `RXB` credit 없이 용기에서 바로 환경으로 방출한다고 모델링했다.
- **용기 외 연료취급 사건:** 정지 후 2.5일, 210일, 310일의 재고를 쓴다. `SFP` 연료취급 사건 셋(DBE%%t:DBE%%, BDBE%%t:BDBE%%, DBA%%t:DBA%%) 가운데 BDBE%%t:BDBE%% 하나만 두 집합체 손상을 반영해 Mechanistic Source Term%%t:MST%%을 두 배로 했다. `SFP` 보유는 `RG 1.183` Appendix B를 따르되 소듐 bond와 물의 반응을 고려해 제염계수(decontamination factor)%%t:DECONTAMINATION_FACTOR%%를 낮췄고, staff는 이를 합리적이라고 보았다. `PIC` 설계가 바뀌어 `ESWR` 사건 해석이 옛 설계에 근거한다는 점이 audit에서 확인되었지만, 연료취급 선원항%%t:SOURCE_TERM%% 적용은 `PSAR`로는 합리적이라고 판단했다. 최종 설계의 `PIC`를 반영한 사건별 선원항%%t:SOURCE_TERM%% 모델은 사업자가 보충 서한에서 OL%%t:OL%% 신청을 위해 개발하겠다고 밝힌 계획이다.
- **용기 외 계통 방출 사건:** `SPS` 누설은 소듐 20 gallons 상당이 `SPS` 셀로 방출된다고 가정한다. 셀 누설률 10 percent와 50 percent는 반감기 약 15시간인 sodium-24가 지배하므로 결과에 큰 영향을 주며, 이 누설률 가정은 비보수적일 수 있어 OL%%t:OL%% 단계에서 다시 평가한다. 커버가스%%t:COVER_GAS%% 방출 DBA%%t:DBA%%는 `HAA`가 여과 없이 2시간에 전량 배기된다고 가정한다. `RWG` DBA%%t:DBA%%는 필터를 우회해 10분 동안 굴뚝으로 방출된다고 가정한다.
