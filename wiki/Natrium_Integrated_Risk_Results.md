# Natrium 통합 위험 평가 결과 (Natrium Overall Plant Risk Performance Summary)

> [!abstract] 요약
> `PSAR` Chapter 4는 `PSAR`용 PRA 범위의 발전소 통합 위험을 NEI 18-04의 세 cumulative risk target%%t:CUM_RISK_TARGET%%과 비교하고, 현재 PRA 결과의 불확실도 없는 평균값으로 site boundary 선량 초과 빈도 `3.12E-03 per plant-year`, `early fatality` 위험 `0 per plant-year`, `latent cancer` 위험 `3.86E-09 per plant-year`를 보고한다. 합산 대상은 Licensing Basis Event%%t:LBE%% 가운데 Design Basis Accident%%t:DBA%%를 빼고 other quantified events%%t:OQE%%를 더한 것이다.

## 원문 사실
- "The overall integrated risk performance of the plant as captured within the scope of the probabilistic risk assessment (PRA) developed for the PSAR, has been evaluated against the three cumulative plant risk performance metrics contained in NEI 18-04, “Modernization of Technical Requirements for Licensing of Advanced Non-Light Water Reactors, Risk-Informed Performance-Based Technology Inclusive Guidance for Non-Light Water Reactor Licensing Basis Development,” Revision 1 (Reference 4.1-1), Section 3.2.2, Task 7b, for risk to the public from radiation." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1 p.4]
- "These metrics are summarized as follows:" [PSAR_ch04 §4.1 p.4]
- "Site Boundary Risk: The total mean frequency of exceeding a site boundary dose of 100 mrem from all licensing basis events (LBEs) and other quantified events (OQEs) should not exceed one per plant-year to comply with the annual cumulative exposure limits in 10 CFR 20." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1 p.4]
- "Exclusion Area Boundary (EAB) Early Fatality Risk: The average individual risk of early fatality within 1 mile of the EAB from all LBEs and OQEs based on mean estimates of frequencies and consequences shall not exceed 5E-07 per plant-year to ensure the NRC safety goal Quantitative Health Objective for early fatality risk is met." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1 p.4]
- "Latent Cancer Risk: The average individual risk of latent cancer fatalities within 10 miles of the EAB from all LBEs and OQEs based on mean estimates of frequencies and consequences shall not exceed 2E-06 per plant-year to ensure the NRC safety goal Quantitative Health Objective for latent cancer fatality risk is met." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1 p.4]
- "For the integrated risk evaluation, “all LBEs” include anticipated operational occurrences (AOOs), design basis events (DBEs), and beyond design basis events (BDBEs); as design basis accidents are addressed deterministically, they are not included in the integrated risk evaluation." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1 p.4]
- "OQEs are events that are quantified outside of the LBE frequency cutoff." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1 p.4]
- "The results are based on mean values, without uncertainties." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1 p.4]
- "The operating license (OL) stage integrated risk evaluation will utilize the full scope PRA, including applicable internal and external hazards." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1 p.4]
- "The integrated performance assessment uses the same considerations and methodologies from the Chapter 3 assessments regarding site characteristics, locations of individual members of the public, source of dose, analysis method, key modeling assumptions, modes of operation, consideration of other radiological sources on the site, and uncertainty and sensitivity." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1 p.4]
- "There is an additional key assumption made that only impacts the cumulative Quantitative Health Objectives (QHOs) results and does not impact the Chapter 3 assessments." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1 p.4]
- "The local boiling source term used for the applicable OQEs assumes that bulk boiling of sodium does not occur." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1 p.4]
- "This assumption may underestimate the calculated QHOs; however, the QHO results documented in PSAR Sections 4.1.1, 4.1.2, and 4.1.3 remain conservative as OQEs are not required to be included in the QHO results." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1 p.4]
- "The software and analytical tools that were used in performing the integrated risk evaluation are the same as those described in Chapter 3." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1 p.4]
- "The limitations in the scope and level of detail of the current PRA are discussed in Section 3.1.1." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1 p.4]
- "Based on the current results of the PRA used for the PSAR, the total mean frequency, without uncertainties, of exceeding a site boundary dose of 100 mrem (1 mSv) from all AOOs, DBEs, BDBEs, and OQEs is 3.12E-03 per plant-year." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1.1 p.5]
- "Therefore, the site boundary dose integrated risk metric is satisfied with margin to the target that the total mean frequency of exceeding a site boundary dose of 100 mrem (1 mSv) from all AOOs, DBEs, BDBEs, and OQEs should not exceed one per plant-year." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1.1 p.5]
- "Based on the current results of the PRA used for the PSAR, the mean frequency, without uncertainties, of the cumulative average individual risk of early fatality within 1 mile of the EAB, summed over all AOOs, DBEs, BDBEs, and OQEs, is 0 per plant-year." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1.2 p.5]
- "Therefore, the EAB early fatality integrated risk metric target of 5E-07 per plant-year is satisfied with margin." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1.2 p.5]
- "Based on the current results of the PRA used for the PSAR, the mean frequency, without uncertainties, of the cumulative average individual risk of latent cancer within 10 miles of the EAB, summed over all AOO, DBEs, BDBEs, and OQEs, is 3.86E-09 per plant-year." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1.3 p.5]
- "Therefore, the latent cancer integrated risk metric target of 2E-06 per plant-year is satisfied with margin." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1.3 p.5]
- "4.1-1 NEI, “Modernization of Technical Requirements for Licensing of Advanced Non-Light Water Reactors, Risk-Informed Performance-Based Technology Inclusive Guidance for Non-Light Water Reactor Licensing Basis Development,” Technical Report 18-04, Revision 1, August 2019 [ADAMS Accession No. ML19241A472]." [PSAR_ch04 §4.1 p.5]
- "The staff compared the cumulative risk metrics in PSAR section 4.1 to the guidance in NEI 18-04 and determined that the metrics were consistent with the exception that OQEs from the PRA were included in the integrated plant performance evaluation in addition to the LBEs required in the LMP methodology." [SER_KU1_CP §4.1.1 p.323]
- "The staff determined that this deviation was acceptable because the content of information in PSAR section 4.1 is consistent with the guidance for the content of construction permit (CP) applications found in section C.4.1 of NEI 21-07 as endorsed by RG 1.253 with certain clarifications, and the inclusion of the OQEs adds additional information in the integrated plant performance evaluation." [SER_KU1_CP §4.1.1 p.323]

## 해설
이 절은 `PSAR`용 PRA 범위 안에서 발전소의 통합 위험 성능을 NEI 18-04 `Task 7b`의 세 cumulative risk target%%t:CUM_RISK_TARGET%%과 비교한다. 첫째 지표는 site boundary 선량 `100 mrem`을 넘는 총 평균 빈도가 `10 CFR 20`의 `annual cumulative exposure limits`를 지키도록 `one per plant-year`를 넘지 않아야 한다는 것이다. 둘째는 Exclusion Area Boundary%%t:EAB%%에서 `1 mile` 안의 `early fatality`의 `average individual risk`이 빈도와 결과의 평균 추정으로 `5E-07 per plant-year`를 넘지 않아 NRC safety goal의 Quantitative Health Objective%%t:QHO%%를 만족하는 것이고, 셋째는 EAB%%t:EAB%%에서 `10 miles` 안의 `latent cancer` 사망의 `average individual risk`이 `2E-06 per plant-year`를 넘지 않는 것이다. `PSAR`은 세 지표 모두의 합산 대상을 모든 LBE%%t:LBE%%와 OQE%%t:OQE%%로 적는다.

통합 위험 평가의 `all LBEs`는 Anticipated Operational Occurrence%%t:AOO%%, Design Basis Event%%t:DBE%%, Beyond Design Basis Event%%t:BDBE%%를 포함하고, DBA%%t:DBA%%는 결정론적으로 다루므로 포함하지 않는다. OQE%%t:OQE%%는 LBE%%t:LBE%% 빈도 cutoff 밖에서 정량화된 사건이다. 결과는 불확실도 없는 평균값에 기반하며, operating license%%t:OL%% 단계의 통합 위험 평가는 해당 내부·외부 재해를 포함한 full scope PRA를 쓸 예정이다. 평가는 부지 특성, 공중 개인의 위치, 선량원, 해석 방법, 핵심 모델링 가정, 운전 모드, 부지의 다른 방사선원 고려, 불확실도와 민감도에 대해 `Chapter 3` 평가와 같은 고려 사항과 방법론을 쓰고, 같은 소프트웨어와 해석 도구를 쓴다. 현재 PRA의 범위와 상세도의 한계는 `Section 3.1.1`에 있다.

누적 QHO%%t:QHO%% 결과에만 영향을 주고 `Chapter 3` 평가에는 영향을 주지 않는 핵심 가정이 하나 더 있다. 해당 OQE%%t:OQE%%에 쓴 local boiling 선원항(source term)%%t:SOURCE_TERM%%은 소듐의 bulk boiling이 일어나지 않는다고 가정한다. `PSAR`은 이 가정이 계산된 QHO%%t:QHO%%를 과소평가할 수 있지만, OQE%%t:OQE%%가 QHO%%t:QHO%% 결과에 포함될 필요가 없으므로 `PSAR Sections 4.1.1, 4.1.2, and 4.1.3`의 결과는 보수적으로 남는다고 적는다.

현재 PRA 결과로, 모든 AOO%%t:AOO%%, DBE%%t:DBE%%, BDBE%%t:BDBE%%, OQE%%t:OQE%%에서 site boundary 선량 `100 mrem (1 mSv)`을 넘는 총 평균 빈도는 불확실도 없이 `3.12E-03 per plant-year`이다. EAB%%t:EAB%%에서 `1 mile` 안 `early fatality`의 누적 `average individual risk`은 `0 per plant-year`, `10 miles` 안 `latent cancer`의 누적 `average individual risk`은 `3.86E-09 per plant-year`이며, `PSAR`은 세 지표가 모두 `satisfied with margin`이라고 적는다. 결과 문장은 위험 값을 `the mean frequency ... of the cumulative average individual risk`로, 목표 문장은 `average individual risk`로 적는다.

NEI 18-04 `Task 7b`의 site boundary 목표 문장은 합산 대상을 모든 LBE%%t:LBE%%로 적고, NEI 18-04 `§4.2.2`는 Structures, Systems, and Components%%t:SSC%% 분류 과정의 cumulative risk limit 기준을 평가할 때 지원 PRA 범위 안의 모든 LBE%%t:LBE%%를 포함해야 한다고 적는다. NEI 18-04의 LBE%%t:LBE%% 정의에는 AOO%%t:AOO%%, DBE%%t:DBE%%, BDBE%%t:BDBE%%와 함께 DBA%%t:DBA%%가 들어 있다. 따라서 `PSAR`의 합산 범위는 OQE%%t:OQE%%를 더하고 DBA%%t:DBA%%를 뺀 점에서 NEI 18-04 문언과 다르다. NEI 18-04는 빈도가 `5E-07/plant-year`보다 낮은 Event Sequence%%t:ES%%를 PRA 결과에 남겨 cliff edge 효과가 없음을 확인하는 데 쓴다고 적는데, 이 사건들과 `PSAR`의 OQE%%t:OQE%%가 같은 범위인지는 vault에 없다.

**NRC staff 평가.** NRC staff는 누적 위험 지표가 NEI 18-04 지침과 일치하되, Licensing Modernization Project%%t:LMP%% 방법론이 요구하는 LBE%%t:LBE%% 외에 PRA의 OQE%%t:OQE%%를 통합 성능 평가에 포함한 점이 예외라고 판단했다. staff는 이 일탈이 `RG 1.253`이 승인한 NEI 21-07 C.4.1의 construction permit%%t:CP%% 신청 내용 지침과 맞고 OQE%%t:OQE%%가 정보를 더하므로 수용 가능하다고 보았다. 따라서 위의 판단 가운데 OQE%%t:OQE%% 포함 부분은 staff 판정과 같고, DBA%%t:DBA%% 제외는 staff가 언급하지 않는다.
