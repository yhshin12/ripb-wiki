# 심층방어 통합 절차 (Integrated Framework for Incorporation and Evaluation of DID)

> [!abstract] 요약
> 원자로 설계자는 integrated decision-making process%%t:IDP%%를 통해 설계의 모든 단계에 심층방어(Defense-in-Depth)%%t:DID%%를 반영하고 그 적절성을 평가하며 baseline을 문서화한다. 그림 5-4의 18개 과업은 개별 과업의 연속이 아니라 반복 과정이고 그 순서는 단계별 절차라기보다 정보 논리를 반영하며, Task 12의 Plant Capability Defense-in-Depth%%t:PLANT_CAPABILITY_DID%% 확인과 Task 17의 Programmatic Defense-in-Depth%%t:PROGRAMMATIC_DID%% 확인이 성공하면 적절성이 확립된다.

## 원문 사실
- "DID is to be considered and incorporated into all phases of defining the design requirements, developing the design, evaluating the design from both deterministic and probabilistic perspectives, and defining the programs to ensure adequate public protection." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 p.60]
- "The reactor designer is responsible for ensuring that DID is achieved through the incorporation of DID features and programs in the design phases and in turn, conducting the evaluation that arrives at the decision of whether adequate DID has been achieved." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 p.60]
- "The reactor designer implements these responsibilities through an Integrated Decision-Making Process (IDP) that guides the overall design effort (including development of plant capability and programmatic DID features), conducts the DID adequacy evaluation of the resulting design, and documents the DID baseline." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 p.60]
- "Note that Figure 5-4 includes many actions described previously by this document and does not imply that these steps need to be re-performed for the purpose of the DID adequacy evaluation." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 p.60]
- "The color coding in the figure identifies elements that are probabilistic, deterministic, and risk-informed (i.e., having both probabilistic and deterministic aspects)." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 p.60]
- "It is emphasized that the implementation of the process is not a series of discrete tasks but rather an iterative process." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 p.60]
- "Iteration through the tasks is expected to continue through the documentation of the DID baseline in Task 18, and then with subsequent DID baseline updates as the design progresses." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 p.60]
- "The sequence of tasks reflects more an information logic than a step-by-step procedure." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 p.60]
- "For advanced non-LWRs that are currently in various stages of design development, the IDP may be implemented by a cross-functional design team responsible for the process tasks shown in Figure 5-4." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 p.62]
- "Top-level requirements are formulated with input from all stakeholders, including owner requirements for such things as energy production, capital costs, operating and maintenance costs, safety, availability, investment protection, siting, and commercialization requirements." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 1 p.62]
- "DID adequacy is given high priority in the early phase of design." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 1 p.62]
- "Owner requirements for plant availability and reliability contribute to protecting the first layer of defense of DID in Figure 5-4 by controlling plant disturbances and preventing some IEs and AOOs." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 1 p.62]
- "The decision whether to use inherent characteristics and passive SSCs as the primary means of assuring PSFs, supplemented by active systems that provide additional layers of defense to the prevention and mitigation of events, is of particular relevance to any design." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 1 p.62]
- "This is a purely deterministic task that produces a definition of the design in sufficient detail to begin the PRA." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 1 p.62]
- "As part of the pre-conceptual design phase, a great deal of the DID capability is naturally established by addressing the fundamental top-level design requirements for operability, availability, maintainability, and investment protection features using conventional practices, industry codes and standards, etc." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 1 p.63]
- "The full extent of special treatment is defined following the evaluation of LBEs and the selection of SSC safety classes for each SSC." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 1 p.63]
- "Hence, selection of codes and standards supports both the plant capabilities for DID and the activities that contribute to the programmatic DID." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 1 p.63]
- "The evaluation of DID adequacy in Tasks 12 and 17 focuses on the LBEs and associated SSCs with the highest levels of risk significance." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 2 p.63]
- "As Task 3 is completed, the plant capabilities that support DID are largely determined." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 3 p.63]
- "Information from the PRA is used together with deterministic inputs to establish DID adequacy as part of the RIPB evaluation of DID depicted in Tasks 12 and 17." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 5 p.64]
- "An important input in evaluating DID adequacy is establishment of adequate margins between the risks of each LBE and the F-C Target." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 7 p.64]
- "Such margins also help demonstrate the level of satisfaction of the NRC’s advanced reactor policy objective of achieving higher margins of safety." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 7 p.64]
- "In addition to establishing adequate margins between the risks of individual LBEs and the F-C Targets, the evaluation of the margins against the cumulative risk metrics identified previously is also necessary to establish DID adequacy." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 8 p.64]
- "The layers of defense process in Figure 5-3 are used in this task to evaluate each LBE with more attention given to risk-significant LBEs to identify and evaluate the DID attributes to support the capabilities in each layer and to minimize dependencies among the layers." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 9 p.64]
- "The selection of SR SSCs is accomplished by examining each of the DBEs and high-consequence BDBEs and by performing sensitivity analyses to determine which of the PSFs modeled in these LBEs are necessary to perform their prevention or mitigation functions to keep the DBEs and high-consequence BDBEs inside the F-C Target." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 10 p.64]
- "DBAs are then constructed, starting with each DBE, and then assuming that only the SR SSCs perform their associated RSFs." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 10 p.64]
- "DID considerations are taken into account in the selection of SR SSCs by selecting those that yield high confidence in performing their functions with sufficient reliability to minimize uncertainties." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 10 p.64]
- "Programmatic DID considerations are taken into account in the formulation of the conservative assumptions for these analyses which need to show that the site boundary doses meet 10 CFR 50.34 acceptance limits." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 11 p.65]
- "As part of the DID adequacy evaluation, each LBE is evaluated to confirm that risk targets are met without exclusive reliance on a single element of design, a single program, or a single DID attribute." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 12 p.65]
- "All the SSCs that participate in a layer of defense are generally not classified as SR." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 13 p.65]
- "However, these SSCs are evaluated against criteria for establishing SSC risk significance and additional criteria for whether the SSC provides a function necessary for DID adequacy." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 13 p.65]
- "None of the NST SSCs are regarded as safety-significant, even though they may contribute to the plant capability for DID." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 13 p.65]
- "One of the primary motivations of employing DID attributes is to address uncertainties, including those that are reflected in the PRA estimates of frequencies and consequence as well as other uncertainties which are not sufficiently characterized for uncertainty quantification nor amenable to sensitivity analyses." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 15 p.65]
- "The layers of defense within a design, including offsite response, are used to compensate for residual unknowns." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 15 p.65]
- "The plant capability DID includes design margins that protect against uncertainties." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 15 p.65]
- "All safety-significant SSCs that are distributed between SR and NSRST are subject to special treatment requirements." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 16 p.65]
- "These include numerical targets for SSC reliability and availability, design margins for performance of the PSFs, and monitoring of performance against these targets with appropriate corrective actions when targets are not fully realized." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 16 p.65]
- "Another consideration in the setting of SSC performance requirements is the need to assure that the results of the plant capability DID evaluation in Task 12 are achieved not just in the design, but in the as-built and as-operated and maintained plant throughout the life of the plant." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 16 p.65]
- "The SSC performance targets are set during the design IDP that is responsible for establishing the adequacy of DID." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 16 p.65]
- "In addition to these performance targets, further special treatments may be identified." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 16 p.65]
- "The adequacy of the programmatic measures for DID is driven by the selection of performance requirements for the safety-significant SSCs in Task 16." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 17 p.66]
- "The programmatic measures are evaluated relative to the risk significance of the SSCs, the roles of SSCs in different layers of defense, and the effectiveness of special treatments in providing additional confidence that the risk-significant SSCs will perform as intended." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 17 p.66]
- "The RIPB evaluation of DID adequacy continues until the recurring evaluation of plant and programmatic DID associated with design and PRA update cycles no longer identifies risk-significant vulnerabilities where potential compensatory actions can make a practical, significant improvement to the LBE risk profiles or risk significant reductions in the level of uncertainty in characterizing the LBE frequencies and consequences." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 18 p.66]
- "The successful outcomes of Tasks 12 and 17 establish DID adequacy." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 18 p.66]
- "This determination is made during the IDP and documented initially in a DID integrated baseline evaluation report which is subsequently revised as the iterations through the design cycles and design evaluation evolve." [NEI_18-04 §5.3 Task 18 p.66]

## 해설
원문은 심층방어%%t:DID%%를 설계 요건 정의, 설계 개발, 결정론적·확률론적 설계 평가, 공중 보호 프로그램 정의의 모든 단계에서 고려하고 반영해야 한다고 적는다. 원자로 설계자는 설계 단계에서 심층방어%%t:DID%% 특성과 프로그램을 반영하고, 적절한 심층방어%%t:DID%%가 달성되었는지 판단하는 평가를 수행할 책임을 진다. 설계자는 이 책임을 IDP%%t:IDP%%로 이행하며, IDP%%t:IDP%%는 전체 설계를 이끌고 결과 설계의 적절성을 평가하며 심층방어%%t:DID%% baseline을 문서화한다. 설계 개발 중인 비경수로에서는 그림 5-4의 과업을 맡는 여러 분야의 설계팀이 IDP%%t:IDP%%를 수행할 수 있다.

그림 5-4의 과업은 앞 장들에서 설명한 활동을 많이 포함하며, 적절성 평가를 위해 이를 다시 수행해야 한다는 뜻은 아니다. 그림의 색 구분은 확률론적 요소, 결정론적 요소, 둘을 겸한 risk-informed 요소를 나타낸다. 원문은 이 절차가 개별 과업의 연속이 아니라 반복 과정이고, 과업 순서는 단계별 절차라기보다 정보의 논리를 반영한다고 강조한다. 반복은 Task 18의 심층방어%%t:DID%% baseline 문서화까지 이어지고, 설계가 진행됨에 따라 baseline 갱신으로 계속된다.

Task 1에서는 모든 이해관계자의 입력으로 에너지 생산, 자본비, 운전·정비비, 안전, 가용도, 투자 보호, 부지, 상용화 같은 소유자 요건을 포함한 최상위 요건을 세우며, 설계 초기부터 심층방어%%t:DID%% 적절성에 높은 우선순위를 둔다. 발전소 가용도·신뢰도에 대한 소유자 요건은 발전소 교란을 억제하고 일부 Initiating Event%%t:IE%%와 Anticipated Operational Occurrence%%t:AOO%%를 막아 첫째 층을 보호하는 데 기여한다. 원문은 고유 특성과 피동 Structures, Systems, and Components%%t:SSC%%를 PRA Safety Function%%t:PSF%% 보장의 주 수단으로 삼고 능동 계통으로 층을 더할지의 결정을 어느 설계에서나 특히 중요하다고 본다. Task 1은 PRA를 시작할 만큼 설계를 정의하는 순수 결정론적 과업이다. 개념 이전 설계 단계에서는 운전성·가용도·정비성·투자 보호에 관한 최상위 설계 요건을 통상의 관행과 산업 코드·표준으로 다루면서 심층방어%%t:DID%% 능력의 상당 부분이 자연히 확립된다. 코드·표준 선택은 Plant Capability Defense-in-Depth%%t:PLANT_CAPABILITY_DID%%와 Programmatic Defense-in-Depth%%t:PROGRAMMATIC_DID%%를 모두 뒷받침하지만, special treatment%%t:SPECIAL_TREATMENT%%의 전체 범위는 Licensing Basis Event%%t:LBE%% 평가와 SSC%%t:SSC%% 안전 등급 선정 뒤에 정해진다. Task 3이 끝나면 심층방어%%t:DID%%를 뒷받침하는 발전소 능력이 대부분 정해진다.

Task 12와 Task 17의 적절성 평가는 risk significance가 가장 높은 LBE%%t:LBE%%와 관련 SSC%%t:SSC%%에 초점을 두며, Task 5의 PRA 정보가 결정론적 입력과 함께 그 평가에 쓰인다. Task 7은 각 LBE%%t:LBE%% 위험과 Frequency-Consequence Target%%t:FC_TARGET%% 사이의 적절한 여유를, Task 8은 누적 위험 지표에 대한 여유를 적절성 평가의 입력으로 삼는다. 원문은 Task 7의 여유가 higher margins of safety를 달성한다는 NRC 신형로 정책 목표의 충족 정도를 보이는 데도 도움이 된다고 적는다. Task 9는 그림 5-3의 Layers of Defense%%t:LAYERS_OF_DEFENSE%% 과정으로 각 LBE%%t:LBE%%를 평가하되 Risk-Significant LBE%%t:RS_LBE%%에 더 주의를 두고, 층마다 능력을 뒷받침하는 심층방어%%t:DID%% attribute를 식별·평가하며 층 사이 의존성을 줄인다.

Task 10은 Design Basis Event%%t:DBE%%와 결말이 큰 Beyond Design Basis Event%%t:BDBE%%를 검토하고 민감도 분석을 수행해 Safety-Related%%t:SR%% SSC%%t:SSC%%를 고르고, 각 DBE%%t:DBE%%에서 SR%%t:SR%% SSC%%t:SSC%%만 기능을 수행한다고 가정해 Design Basis Accident%%t:DBA%%를 구성한다. 이때 심층방어%%t:DID%% 고려로 불확실도를 줄일 만큼 충분한 신뢰도로 기능을 수행한다는 높은 확신을 주는 설비를 고른다. Task 11의 DBA%%t:DBA%% 보수적 결정론 해석에서는 Programmatic Defense-in-Depth%%t:PROGRAMMATIC_DID%% 고려가 보수적 가정에 반영되며, 해석은 부지 경계 선량이 10 CFR 50.34 허용 한계를 만족함을 보여야 한다. Task 12는 각 LBE%%t:LBE%%가 설계 요소 하나, 프로그램 하나, 심층방어%%t:DID%% attribute 하나에만 의존하지 않고 위험 목표를 만족하는지 확인한다. Task 13에서는 층에 참여하는 SSC%%t:SSC%%가 대체로 SR%%t:SR%%로 분류되지 않지만 risk significance 기준과 심층방어%%t:DID%% 적절성에 필요한 기능인지에 관한 기준으로 평가되어 Non-Safety-Related with Special Treatment%%t:NSRST%% SSC%%t:SSC%%가 식별된다. Non-Safety-Related with No Special Treatment%%t:NST%% SSC%%t:SSC%%는 발전소 능력에 기여할 수 있더라도 safety-significant로 보지 않는다. Task 14는 Required Functional Design Criteria%%t:RFDC%%를 다루며, 내용은 [[Required_Functional_Design_Criteria]]에 있다.

Task 15에서 원문은 심층방어%%t:DID%% attribute를 쓰는 주된 동기로, PRA 빈도·결말 추정에 반영된 불확실도뿐 아니라 정량화나 민감도 분석이 어려운 불확실도까지 다루는 것을 든다. 부지 밖 대응을 포함한 Layers of Defense%%t:LAYERS_OF_DEFENSE%%는 남은 미지 요소를 보상하는 데 쓰이며, Plant Capability Defense-in-Depth%%t:PLANT_CAPABILITY_DID%%는 불확실도에 대비하는 design margin%%t:DESIGN_MARGIN%%을 포함한다. Task 16은 SR%%t:SR%%과 NSRST%%t:NSRST%%로 나뉜 모든 Safety-Significant SSC%%t:SS_SSC%%에 special treatment%%t:SPECIAL_TREATMENT%% 요건을 부과하며, 여기에는 신뢰도·가용도 수치 목표, PSF%%t:PSF%% 수행의 design margin%%t:DESIGN_MARGIN%%, 목표 대비 성능 감시와 목표 미달 시의 시정 조치가 포함된다. 성능 요건을 정할 때는 Task 12의 평가 결과가 설계에서뿐 아니라 건설·운전·정비된 발전소에서 수명 동안 달성되도록 보장할 필요도 고려하며, 성능 목표는 적절성 확립을 맡은 설계 IDP%%t:IDP%%에서 정한다. 성능 목표 외에 special treatment%%t:SPECIAL_TREATMENT%%가 더 식별될 수 있다. Task 17의 Programmatic Defense-in-Depth%%t:PROGRAMMATIC_DID%% 적절성은 Task 16의 성능 요건 선정에 따라 좌우되며, 프로그램 조치는 SSC%%t:SSC%%의 risk significance, 여러 층에서 SSC%%t:SSC%%가 맡는 역할, risk-significant SSC%%t:SSC%%가 의도대로 작동한다는 확신을 더하는 special treatment%%t:SPECIAL_TREATMENT%%의 효과에 비추어 평가한다. risk-informed and performance-based%%t:RIPB%% 평가는 설계·PRA 갱신 주기와 함께 반복되는 평가가 compensatory action%%t:COMPENSATORY_ACTION%%으로 LBE%%t:LBE%% 위험 프로파일을 실질적으로 크게 개선하거나 빈도·결말 불확실도를 크게 줄일 수 있는 risk-significant 취약점을 더는 찾지 못할 때까지 계속된다. Task 12와 Task 17의 성공적 결과가 심층방어%%t:DID%% 적절성을 확립하며, 이 판단은 IDP%%t:IDP%%에서 내려져 심층방어%%t:DID%% 통합 baseline 평가 보고서에 처음 기록되고 설계 반복에 따라 개정된다.
