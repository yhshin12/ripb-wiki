# NRC의 Natrium CP 단계 PRA 평가 (NRC Evaluation of the Natrium CP-Stage PRA)

> [!abstract] 요약
> NRC staff는 `RG 1.253` Appendix A와 `RG 1.247`의 해당 입장을 적용해 Kemmerer Unit 1의 construction permit%%t:CP%% 단계 PRA를 검토했다. PRA 요소 열두 개 각각을 "reasonable"로 판정했다. 근거는 `PSAR` 서술이 `RG 1.253`의 CP%%t:CP%% 단계 지침에 맞고 해석이 `RG 1.247` 지침에 따라 수행되었다는 것이다. 이 PRA는 내부 기시사건만 다루며, 여러 요소에서 근거 보강과 재평가를 operating license%%t:OL%% 단계로 넘겼다.

## 원문 사실
- "The staff followed the guidance in Appendix A of RG 1.253, “Guidance for a Technology-Inclusive Content-of-Application Methodology to Inform the Licensing Basis and Content of Applications for Licenses, Certifications, and Approvals for Non-Light-Water Reactors,” Rev. 0 (ML23269A222) to review the KU1 CP stage PRA within the context of the LMP framework." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.1 p.152]
- "Appendix A provides guidance on the expected scope, level of detail, and quality of a PRA suitable for the CP stage, recognizing that the plant design is still evolving and that the PRA model is inherently limited in maturity." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.1 p.152]
- "Because the KU1 PRA is still preliminary and currently addresses only internal initiating events (IEs), the staff applied applicable positions in RG 1.247" [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.1 p.153]
- "which endorses, with clarifications, the American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME) and American Nuclear Society (ANS) RA-S-1.4-2021 non-light-water reactor (non-LWR) PRA standard" [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.1 p.153]
- "As part of its review, the staff conducted both a virtual audit and a three-day in-person audit focused on the PRA and its supporting documentation." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.156]
- "The staff’s audit included the KU1 PRA model that the applicant developed using the Computer Aided Fault Tree Analysis System (CAFTA) software." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.156]
- "The staff also conducted an audit of selected sensitivity analyses to assess the robustness of the plant design at the CP stage and to better understand the uncertainties inherent in the PRA modeling." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.156]
- "The KU1 PRA demonstrates that the risk associated with the KU1 site and preliminary Natrium design is acceptably low and provides meaningful risk insights in support of implementing the LMP methodology described in NEI 18-04." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.156]
- "These consequence-based metrics are aligned with those used in LWR level 3 PRAs but exclude consideration of radiological or other impacts on on-site personnel." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.157]
- "Based on its review, the staff determined these source terms to be reasonable and sufficient for the CP stage PRA" [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.158]
- "Based on its review, the staff determined the POS analysis to be reasonable because the information in PSAR section 3.1.1.6 describing the analysis is consistent with RG 1.253 guidance for CP applications and the analysis was performed in accordance with RG 1.247 guidance applicable to the CP stage" [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.158]
- "LPSD evolutions were segmented into separate POSs based on differences in plant response to IEs." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.158]
- "Many of the IE frequencies were reasonably estimated." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.160]
- "additional documentation of the SC for each safety-significant PSF reflecting the final design, including quantification and uncertainty characterization will be established at the OL stage." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.162]
- "Support systems and their dependencies were represented within the models. However, at the CP stage, assumptions were made around the availability and capability of these systems that will need verification at the OL stage." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.164]
- "Due to the preliminary nature of the design and unavailability of design information, some SSCs are not modeled within the CP stage PRA. The incorporation of additional SSCs as the design progresses may increase the frequency of some events because the additional SSCs may introduce new modes of failure." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.164]
- "No credit was taken for recovery actions." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.166]
- "The staff notes that additional information on the bases of the failure probabilities selected is expected at the OL stage." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.167]
- "The staff expects USO to reassess the HS assumptions, sources of modeling, and completeness uncertainty at the OL stage, based on final design information, and either confirm their continued validity or update the HS analysis as necessary." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.168]
- "PRA quantification was conducted using a single top gate approach for each event sequence and release category." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.168]
- "Bounding source terms were calculated to account for uncertainties inherent in the source term estimation process." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.170]
- "The MACCS code was used to perform the RC calculations." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.171]
- "Both mean values and 95th percentile estimates were developed, based on analyses of nominal and bounding source terms to ensure a robust evaluation." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.171]
- "Other internal and external hazards (e.g., internal and external fires, internal and external flooding, seismic events, high winds, tornadoes) were not included in this integration, which is consistent with Appendix A of RG 1.253." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.172]
- "USO is expected to fully develop the SF PRA at the OL stage, including updating assumptions, system modeling, and uncertainty characterizations, as necessary based on final design information." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.174]
- "Portions of the self-assessment appeared to have been performed by individuals with limited PRA expertise, which could lead to varying interpretations of the PRA supporting requirements and potentially influence the overall conclusions and grading outcomes." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.176]
- "While recognizing that the KU1 PRA self-assessment serves as a valuable initial tool for evaluating PRA quality, the staff does not rely solely on it to determine PRA adequacy or acceptability, even for PRA supporting requirements assessed as “Met.”" [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.176]
- "In PSAR section 3.1.1.2, USO stated that a PRA peer review will be conducted to support the OL stage PRA, which will replace the KU1 PRA self-assessment and provide an additional level of confidence in the PRA model and results." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.176]
- "Overall, the staff determined the KU1 PRA configuration control procedure to be reasonably developed." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.3 p.177]
- "The staff concludes that the PRA information described in the PSAR conforms to the guidance documents, referenced above, and that the scope, level of detail, and quality of the KU1 PRA are reasonable for the CP application. This conclusion is based on the understanding that the KU1 PRA is preliminary to support the CP application, and that the applicant is not requesting finality at this stage." [SER_KU1_CP §3.1.4 p.185]

## 해설
staff는 PRA를 `RG 1.253` Appendix A의 CP%%t:CP%% 단계 기대(범위, 상세도, 품질)에 비추어 검토했다. 이 PRA가 내부 기시사건만 다루므로, 비경수형원전(non-light-water reactor)%%t:NON_LWR%% PRA 표준을 단서와 함께 승인하는 `RG 1.247`의 해당 입장을 함께 적용했다. 검토의 일부로 가상 audit과 3일간의 현장 audit을 했고, `CAFTA`로 만든 PRA 모델과 선택된 민감도 해석도 audit 대상이었다.

staff는 PRA 요소 열두 개와 사용후연료 PRA를 각각 "reasonable"로 판정했다. 판정 문장은 대체로 `PSAR`의 해당 서술이 `RG 1.253`의 CP%%t:CP%% 단계 지침에 맞는다는 근거를 들며, Plant Operating State%%t:POS%% 판정처럼 해석이 `RG 1.247` 지침에 따라 수행되었다는 근거를 함께 드는 경우가 있다. 동시에 요소별 한계와 OL%%t:OL%% 단계 과제를 적었다.
- 일부 Structures, Systems, and Components%%t:SSC%%는 설계 정보가 없어 모델링되지 않았다. 이들이 추가되면 새 고장 모드 때문에 일부 사건의 빈도가 커질 수 있다.
- 지원 계통의 가용성과 능력에 관한 가정은 OL%%t:OL%% 단계에서 확인해야 한다.
- 인적 신뢰도 분석에서는 복구 조치를 credit하지 않았다.
- 고장 확률 선택의 근거는 OL%%t:OL%% 단계에서 추가로 제시되어야 한다.
- 위험요인 스크리닝(`HS`)의 가정과 불완전성 불확실성은 최종 설계로 재평가해야 한다.

위험도 통합은 `RG 1.253` Appendix A와 일관되게 내부·외부 화재, 침수, 지진, 강풍, 토네이도 같은 다른 위험요인을 포함하지 않았다. 불확실성을 반영하려고 nominal 선원항(source term)%%t:SOURCE_TERM%%과 함께 bounding 선원항%%t:SOURCE_TERM%%도 계산했고, 방사선 영향 계산은 `MACCS`로 두 선원항%%t:SOURCE_TERM%%에 대해 mean과 95th percentile을 산출했다.

자체평가에 대해 staff는 일부가 PRA 전문성이 제한된 인력으로 수행되었을 수 있다고 지적했다. 또 자체평가를 유용한 초기 도구로 인정하면서도, "Met"로 평가된 요구사항이라도 PRA 적정성 판단을 자체평가에만 의존하지 않는다고 밝혔다. 사업자는 OL%%t:OL%% 단계 PRA를 위해 peer review를 하겠다고 밝혔고, PRA 형상관리 절차는 합리적으로 개발되었다고 판단되었다.

staff의 결론은 PRA의 범위, 상세도, 품질이 CP%%t:CP%% 신청에 합리적이라는 것이다. 이 결론은 PRA가 예비적이고 사업자가 이 단계에서 finality를 요청하지 않는다는 이해에 근거한다. OL%%t:OL%% 단계 기대 사항은 [[Natrium_SER_PRA_OL_Expectations]]에 정리했다.
