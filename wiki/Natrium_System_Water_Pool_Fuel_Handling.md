# Natrium Water Pool Fuel Handling System (FHP)

> [!abstract] 요약
> `PSAR` Chapter 7의 Water Pool Fuel Handling System(`FHP`)은 spent fuel과 조사된 비핵연료 노심 집합체를 물 환경에 저장하며 Spent Fuel Pool, Pool Immersion Cell, Fuel Pool Cooling, Fuel Pool Purification으로 이루어진다. Safety-Related%%t:SR%% 피동 열제거·barrier%%t:BARRIER%% 기능과 Non-Safety-Related with Special Treatment%%t:NSRST%% 비상 보충수·radionuclide boundary·사고 후 감시 기능을 지원한다.

## 원문 사실
- "Fuel handling activities are supported by the Ex-Vessel Fuel Handling System described in Section 7.3.2, the In-Vessel Fuel Handling System described in Section 7.3.3, and the Water Pool Fuel Handling System (FHP) described in this section." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3 p.164]
- "These systems encompass the fuel handling operations from the reactor core to the SFP." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3 p.164]
- "The FHP provides a water-based environment for the intermediate-term storage of spent fuel, and is used for the storage of non-fuel irradiated core assemblies prior to disposal." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.1 p.164]
- "The FHP provides core assembly cooling, spent fuel reactivity control, fission product containment, and radiation shielding." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.1 p.164]
- "The FHP is divided into four subsystems: Spent Fuel Pool (SFP), Pool Immersion Cell (PIC), Fuel Pool Cooling (FPC), and Fuel Pool Purification (FPP)." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.1 p.164]
- "The spent fuel pool criticality analysis is described in Section 3.14." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.1 p.164]
- "The PIC is the system that transitions core assemblies being stored in a sodium environment to a water-filled environment." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.1 p.165]
- "During the cleaning process, heat is passively transferred through the cleaning vessel to the SFP water." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.1 p.165]
- "The FPC is classified as non-safety-related with no special treatment." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.1 p.165]
- "The FPC is designed to maintain the SFP water temperature within prescribed limits by transferring decay heat generated from core assemblies to the NI Water System." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.1 p.165]
- "The FPP is classified as non-safety-related with no special treatment." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.1 p.165]
- "The compliance with regulatory criteria, identification of design criteria, and codes and standards that define the FHP design basis are described below." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.166]
- "Principal design criteria are identified in Section 5.3." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.166]
- "Compliance with Principal Design Criteria (PDC) that are applicable to the FHP is described below." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.166]
- "Consistent with PDC 2, Design bases for protection against natural phenomena, the FHP structures, systems, and components (SSCs) are designed, based on the appropriate combination of the effects of normal and accident conditions with the effects of external hazards and natural phenomena, to maintain capability to perform safety-significant functions." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.166]
- "The methods applied for the evaluation of natural phenomena and the determination of associated design basis hazard levels are described in Section 6.1.1." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.166]
- "The handling equipment, storage racks, and SFP structure are designed to withstand earthquakes using methods and criteria described in Section 6.4.1." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.166]
- "The FHP is protected by the FHB, described in Section 7.8.2, from external flooding and precipitation hazards." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.166]
- "The SFP is designed to account for the effects of extreme winds and wind-related missiles." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.166]
- "The operation of safety-significant FHP equipment is administratively controlled to prevent operation during extreme wind events." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.166]
- "Consistent with PDC 3, Fire Protection, the FHP core assembly handling equipment and cleaning process are designed to contain assembly residual sodium to limit the potential for fires." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.166]
- "The FHP is located within the FHB in dedicated spaces for core assembly handling and storage activities to minimize the potential impact to other safety-significant SSCs in the event of a fire." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.166]
- "The FHP equipment is constructed of noncombustible and non-flammable materials to the extent practical." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.166]
- "Fire detection and protection is provided by the NI Fire Protection System, as described in Section 7.5.2." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.166]
- "Consistent with PDC 4, Environment and dynamic effects design bases, the FHP SSCs are designed to accommodate the effects of, and to be compatible with applicable environmental conditions of spent fuel decay heat and radiation, liquid sodium and it's aerosols, vapors, and oxidation products." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.166]
- "Equipment qualification for harsh environmental conditions is provided by the component quality, design, and qualification requirements in accordance with the Equipment Qualification Program described in Chapter 8." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.166]
- "The FHP is protected against dynamic effects, including the effects of discharging fluids that may result from equipment failures and external events." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.166]
- "The FHP SSCs are designed using materials that are selected to be capable of withstanding the operating environments to which they are exposed." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.166]
- "Consistent with PDC 61, Fuel storage and handling and radioactivity control, the design of the FHP provides adequate SFP water inventory for stored fuel residual heat removal and provides suitable shielding for radiation protection under normal and postulated accident conditions." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.167]
- "FHP fuel storage and handling equipment provides safe containment and confinement of core assemblies and is designed to permit periodic inspection and testing to assure safety-significant functional capability." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.167]
- "Consistent with PDC 62, Prevention of criticality in fuel storage and handling, criticality in the fuel storage and handling system is prevented in accordance with 10 CFR 50.68(b) and the criticality analysis is described in Section 3.14." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.167]
- "Criticality in the SFP fuel storage area is prevented by fixed borated stainless steel neutron absorber plates in the fuel region of the storage racks and by having a subcritical geometry configuration that maintains criticality safety within the fuel storage areas during normal and accident conditions." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.167]
- "Consistent with PDC 63, Monitoring fuel and waste storage, SFP level and temperature instrumentation and FHB radiation monitoring is provided to detect conditions that may result in loss of residual heat removal capability and excessive radiation levels and to initiate appropriate safety actions." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.167]
- "Consistent with PDC 74, Sodium and water reaction prevention and mitigation, the FHP is designed to remove sodium from core assemblies utilizing a humidified inert gas stream in a controlled environment within the PIC cleaning vessel prior to immersion in the SFP." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.167]
- "Probabilistic risk assessment (PRA) safety functions are determined through the plant-specific PRA described in Section 3.1." [PSAR_ch07 §7.3.1.2 p.167]

## 해설
`PSAR` Chapter 7의 핵연료 취급 계통은 `§7.3.2` Ex-Vessel Fuel Handling System, `§7.3.3` In-Vessel Fuel Handling System, 이 절의 `FHP`로 이루어지며, 원자로 노심에서 `SFP`까지의 취급 운전을 포괄한다. `FHP`는 spent fuel의 `intermediate-term storage`와 폐기 전 비핵연료 조사 노심 집합체 저장을 위한 물 환경을 제공하고, 노심 집합체 냉각, spent fuel 반응도 제어, 핵분열생성물 격납, 방사선 차폐를 담당한다. 하위 계통은 Spent Fuel Pool(`SFP`), Pool Immersion Cell(`PIC`), Fuel Pool Cooling(`FPC`), Fuel Pool Purification(`FPP`)이며, `FPC`와 `FPP`는 Non-Safety-Related with No Special Treatment%%t:NST%%로 분류된다. `PIC`는 소듐 환경에 저장되던 노심 집합체를 물 환경으로 옮기는 계통이고, 세정 중 열은 세정 용기를 거쳐 `SFP` 물로 피동 전달된다.

principal design criteria%%t:PDC%% 적용에서 `FHP` Structures, Systems, and Components%%t:SSC%%는 PDC%%t:PDC%% 2에 따라 정상·사고 조건과 외부 재해·자연 현상 영향의 조합에 대해 safety-significant 기능 수행 능력을 유지하도록 설계되고, 취급 설비·저장 rack·`SFP` 구조물은 `Section 6.4.1`의 방법으로 지진을 견디도록 설계되며, 외부 침수와 강수로부터는 `FHB`가 보호한다. `SFP`는 `extreme winds`와 wind-related missile의 영향을 고려해 설계되고, safety-significant `FHP` 설비는 극한 풍 사건 중 운전하지 않도록 행정적으로 관리된다. PDC%%t:PDC%% 3에 따라 취급 설비와 세정 공정은 잔류 소듐을 담아 화재 가능성을 제한한다. PDC%%t:PDC%% 61에 따라 `FHP` 설계는 정상 조건과 가정 사고 조건에서 잔열 제거에 충분한 `SFP` 물 재고와 방사선 차폐를 제공한다. PDC%%t:PDC%% 62에 따라 임계는 `10 CFR 50.68(b)`에 맞춰 방지되며, `SFP` 저장 영역에서는 저장 rack의 고정 borated stainless steel 중성자 흡수판과 subcritical geometry 배치로 방지된다. PDC%%t:PDC%% 63에 따라 `SFP` 수위·온도 계측과 `FHB` 방사선 감시가 제공되고, PDC%%t:PDC%% 74에 따라 `PIC` cleaning vessel에서 가습 불활성 기체로 소듐을 제거한 뒤 `SFP`에 담근다.

`FHP`가 지원하는 safety-significant 기능은 SR%%t:SR%% 기능 `DL3-HR10`(`Passive Heat Removal in FHP`), `DL3-HR13`(`Passive Heat Removal in the FFC`), `DL3-RR8`(`Failed Fuel Canister Barrier`), `DL3-RR5a`(`BLTC Transfer Barrier`)와 NSRST%%t:NSRST%% 기능 `DL4-HR4`(`FHP Emergency Makeup`), `DL2-RR2`(`PIC Radionuclide Boundary`), `DL5-PAM1`(`Post Accident Monitoring`)이다. 정상 냉각을 쓸 수 없으면 노심 집합체는 `SFP` 안의 자연 순환으로 피동 냉각되고, 비상 보충수는 `FHB` 밖 연결에서 전원 없이 exterior fill leg로 공급된다. `DL3-RR5a`와 `DL2-RR2`는 fueling floor valve, `PIC` cleaning vessel, `PIC` Receiver, processing skid 구성품을 함께 쓰며, 앞의 것은 `BLTC`에서 `PIC`로 옮기는 동안 `BLTC` barrier가 containment barrier를 이루고 `BLTC`에서 연료가 낙하할 때 `PIC`가 `BLTC`와 함께 radionuclide boundary를 이루는 기능이며, 뒤의 것은 노심 집합체가 `PIC`에 있을 때 Anticipated Operational Occurrence%%t:AOO%% 중 노심 집합체와 `FHB` 환경 사이의 radionuclide boundary이다.

intact·failed fuel rack은 노심 집합체를 구조적으로 지지하고 하중 경로는 `SFP` liner를 거쳐 SR%%t:SR%% `FHB` substructure로 이어지며, `PHM`과 `PIC`는 연료 이동 중 임시 하중 경로이다. `RG 1.13` 부합은 부분 부합으로, 정상 보충수가 `Seismic Category I`이 아니고 heat sink 설계 하중이 원자로 열출력의 `0.3%` 이상이 아니며(설계상 전 노심 offload를 `SFP`로 할 수 없음, `precludes a full core offload`), 각 예외는 운전원 조치 전 `7 days` 넘게 피동 냉각할 수 있다는 해석으로 뒷받침된다. `SFP`는 정상 운전 `ten years`분의 spent fuel과 다른 노심 집합체를 저장하는 크기이고, 물 부피는 가득 찬 `SFP` 열부하(약 `225 kilowatts`)에서 보충 없이 적어도 `7 days` 냉각하며, 총 물 재고는 약 `574,000 gallons`이다. `Table 7.3.1-1`에서 fuel rack, liner, `PIC` failed fuel canister는 SR%%t:SR%%·`SCS1`, `PIC` cleaning vessel·process skid·receiver는 SR%%t:SR%%·`SCS2`, exterior fill leg·수위·온도 계측·`PHM`은 NSRST%%t:NSRST%%·`SCN3`이다. `neutron absorber material monitoring program`은 operating license%%t:OL%% 단계에서 제공될 예정이다(`will`). `RG 1.240`은 완전 부합이고, `RG 1.97`은 부분 부합으로 `10 CFR 50.155(e)` 요건을 충족하는 데 쓰인다. `PHM`은 지진 뒤 운전할 필요는 없으나 `SFP` 위에 걸쳐 있으므로 `SFP` 안전 기능을 해치거나 하중을 떨어뜨려서는 안 된다(`must not`).

`PSAR` Chapter 5 표는 이 계통을 한 곳에서 `Water Fuel Pool Handling System`, 다른 곳에서 `Water Pool Fuel Handling System`으로 적고, Chapter 7은 뒤의 이름을 쓴다. Chapter 5는 `DL4-HR4`를 `Risk-Significant Function`으로 표시하고, Chapter 7은 그 기능의 exterior fill leg를 `SCN3`로 분류하며, Chapter 6은 `SCN3`를 NSRST%%t:NSRST%% SSC%%t:SSC%%의 최소 초기 분류로, `SCN1`을 지진 위험(`seismic risk`)에 대한 기여 가능성이 높다고 식별된 SSC%%t:SSC%%에 부여한다고 적는다. Chapter 7은 exterior fill leg가 지진·비지진 설계기준 사건 중 기능하도록 설계된다고도 적는다. `DL4-HR4`의 `Risk-Significant Function` 표시와 exterior fill leg의 `SCN3` 분류의 관계를 설명하는 원문은 vault에 없다. Chapter 6은 `SCS2`를 지진 시 고장이 safety significant function에 악영향을 주지 않는 SR%%t:SR%% SSC%%t:SSC%%에 부여한다고 적는다.
