# SFR-DC 전체 요건 (SFR-DC Overall Requirements, Criteria 1–5)

> [!abstract] 요약
> sodium-cooled fast reactor design criteria의 전체 요건은 품질 기준과 기록, 자연 현상 방호, 화재 방호, 환경·동적 영향 설계기준, 구조물·계통·기기 공유를 다룬다. 기준 1, 2, 5는 general design criteria%%t:GDC%%와 같고 기준 3은 advanced reactor design criteria와 같으며, 기준 4는 Anticipated Operational Occurrence%%t:AOO%%의 환경 조건, 액체 소듐과 그 에어로졸·산화 생성물의 영향, 소듐 누설 같은 화학적 결말을 더해 수정되었다.

## 원문 사실
- "Structures, systems, and components important to safety shall be designed, fabricated, erected, and tested to quality standards commensurate with the importance of the safety functions to be performed. Where generally recognized codes and standards are used, they shall be identified and evaluated to determine their applicability, adequacy, and sufficiency and shall be supplemented or modified as necessary to assure a quality product in keeping with the required safety function. A quality assurance program shall be established and implemented in order to provide adequate assurance that these structures, systems, and components will satisfactorily perform their safety functions. Appropriate records of the design, fabrication, erection, and testing of structures, systems, and components important to safety shall be maintained by or under the control of the nuclear power unit licensee throughout the life of the unit." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 1 p.63]
- "Structures, systems, and components important to safety shall be designed to withstand the effects of natural phenomena such as earthquakes, tornadoes, hurricanes, floods, tsunami, and seiches without loss of capability to perform their safety functions. The design bases for these structures, systems, and components shall reflect: (1) Appropriate consideration of the most severe of the natural phenomena that have been historically reported for the site and surrounding area, with sufficient margin for the limited accuracy, quantity, and period of time in which the historical data have been accumulated, (2) appropriate combinations of the effects of normal and accident conditions with the effects of the natural phenomena and (3) the importance of the safety functions to be performed." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 2 p.63]
- "Structures, systems, and components important to safety shall be designed and located to minimize, consistent with other safety requirements, the probability and effect of fires and explosions. Noncombustible and fire-resistant materials shall be used wherever practical throughout the unit, particularly in locations with structures, systems, or components important to safety. Fire detection and fighting systems of appropriate capacity and capability shall be provided and designed to minimize the adverse effects of fires on structures, systems, and components important to safety. Firefighting systems shall be designed to ensure that their rupture or inadvertent operation does not significantly impair the safety capability of these structures, systems, and components." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 3 p.64]
- "The phrase containing examples where noncombustible and fire-resistant materials must be used has been broadened to apply to all advanced reactor designs." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 3 p.64]
- "Instead of “and,” the phrase “locations with structures, systems, and components (SSCs) important to safety” uses “or,” which is logically correct in this case." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 3 p.64]
- "Structures, systems, and components important to safety shall be designed and located to minimize, consistent with other safety requirements, the probability and effect of fires and explosions. Noncombustible and fire- resistant materials shall be used wherever practical throughout the unit, particularly in locations with structures, systems, or components important to safety. Fire detection and fighting systems of appropriate capacity and capability shall be provided and designed to minimize the adverse effects of fires on structures, systems, and components important to safety. Firefighting systems shall be designed to ensure that their rupture or inadvertent operation does not significantly impair the safety capability of these structures, systems, and components." [RG_1.232 ARDC 3 p.31]
- "Structures, systems, and components important to safety shall be designed to accommodate the effects of, and to be compatible with, the environmental conditions associated with normal operation, maintenance, testing, anticipated operational occurrences, and postulated accidents, including the effects of liquid sodium and its aerosols and oxidation products. These structures, systems, and components shall be appropriately protected against dynamic effects, including the effects of missiles, pipe whipping, and discharging fluids that may result from equipment failures and from events and conditions outside the nuclear power unit. However, dynamic effects associated with postulated pipe ruptures in nuclear power units may be excluded from the design basis when analyses reviewed and approved by the Commission demonstrate that the probability of fluid system piping rupture is extremely low under conditions consistent with the design basis for the piping." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 4 p.64]
- "Chemical consequences of accidents, such as sodium leakage, shall be appropriately considered for the design of structures, systems, and components important to safety, which must be protected." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 4 p.65]
- "This change removes the light-water reactor (LWR) emphasis on loss-of-coolant accidents (LOCAs) that may not apply to every design. For example, helium is not needed in a MHTGR to remove heat from the core during postulated accidents and does not have the same importance as water does for LWR designs to ensure that fuel integrity is maintained. Therefore, a specific reference to LOCAs is not applicable to all designs. LOCAs may still require analysis in conjunction with postulated accidents if relevant to the design." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 4 p.64]
- "The phrase “the environmental conditions associated with anticipated operational occurrences” has been added to ensure that the criterion would apply to all SFR design-basis events, as suggested in NUREG-1368." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 4 p.64]
- "A new sentence is added to ensure the designer considers the effects of sodium leakage and associated chemical reactions with SSCs important to safety, which must be protected." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 4 p.64]
- "Structures, systems, and components important to safety shall be designed to accommodate the effects of and to be compatible with the environmental conditions associated with normal operation, maintenance, testing, and postulated accidents. These structures, systems, and components, shall be appropriately protected against dynamic effects, including the effects of missiles, pipe whipping, and discharging fluids, that may result from equipment failures and from events and conditions outside the nuclear power unit. However, dynamic effects associated with postulated pipe ruptures in nuclear power units may be excluded from the design basis when analyses reviewed and approved by the Commission demonstrate that the probability of fluid system piping rupture is extremely low under conditions consistent with the design basis for the piping." [RG_1.232 ARDC 4 p.31]
- "Structures, systems, and components important to safety shall not be shared among nuclear power units unless it can be shown that such sharing will not significantly impair their ability to perform their safety functions, including, in the event of an accident in one unit, an orderly shutdown and cooldown of the remaining units." [RG_1.232 SFR-DC 5 p.65]

## 해설
기준 1은 important to safety Structures, Systems, and Components%%t:SSC%%를 수행할 안전 기능의 중요도에 상응하는 품질 기준으로 설계·제작·설치·시험하고, 일반적으로 인정된 코드·표준을 쓸 때 적용성·적절성·충분성을 평가해 필요하면 보완하거나 수정하도록 요구한다. 또 품질보증 프로그램을 수립·이행하고, 설계·제작·설치·시험 기록을 호기 수명 동안 유지하게 한다. 기준 2는 지진, 토네이도, 허리케인, 홍수, 쓰나미, 세이시 같은 자연 현상의 영향에도 안전 기능 수행 능력을 잃지 않도록 설계하게 하며, 설계기준에는 부지와 주변에서 역사적으로 보고된 가장 심한 현상에 자료의 정확도·양·기간의 한계를 고려한 충분한 여유를 둔 값, 정상·사고 조건의 영향과 자연 현상 영향의 조합, 안전 기능의 중요도를 반영하게 한다. 기준 5는 한 호기에서 사고가 났을 때 나머지 호기의 질서 있는 정지와 냉각을 포함해 안전 기능 수행 능력을 크게 해치지 않음이 입증되지 않으면 important to safety SSC%%t:SSC%%를 호기 사이에 공유하지 못하게 한다. 세 기준은 표 1에서 GDC%%t:GDC%%와 같다.

기준 3은 important to safety SSC%%t:SSC%%를 다른 안전 요건과 일관되게 화재와 폭발의 확률과 영향을 최소화하도록 설계·배치하고, 불연성·내화성 재료를 가능한 곳마다 쓰며, 적절한 용량과 능력의 화재 탐지·진화 계통을 두되 그 파열이나 의도치 않은 작동이 안전 능력을 크게 해치지 않도록 요구한다. 표 1 상태는 advanced reactor design criteria와 같음이다. 근거는 불연성·내화성 재료를 써야 하는 장소의 예시 문구를 모든 신형로 설계에 적용되도록 넓혔고, SSC%%t:SSC%%가 있는 장소를 가리키는 문구에서 논리적으로 맞는 or를 썼다고 설명한다.

기준 4는 수정된 기준이다. advanced reactor design criteria의 같은 번호 기준은 정상 운전, 정비, 시험, 가정 사고의 환경 조건을 적는데, 이 기준은 여기에 AOO%%t:AOO%%의 환경 조건과 액체 소듐, 그 에어로졸과 산화 생성물의 영향을 더하고, 소듐 누설 같은 사고의 화학적 결말을 보호해야 할 important to safety SSC%%t:SSC%% 설계에서 적절히 고려하라는 문장을 추가한다. 미사일, 배관 휩, 방출 유체 같은 동적 영향으로부터의 보호와, 배관 파열 확률이 극히 낮음이 승인된 해석으로 입증되면 가정 배관 파열의 동적 영향을 설계기준에서 뺄 수 있다는 문장은 유지된다. 근거는 모든 설계에 해당하지 않을 수 있는 경수로의 loss of coolant accident%%t:LOCA%% 강조를 없앴고, LOCA%%t:LOCA%%는 설계에 관련되면 가정 사고와 함께 여전히 분석이 필요할 수 있다고 적는다. AOO%%t:AOO%% 문구는 기준이 모든 sodium-cooled fast reactor%%t:SFR%% 설계기준 사건에 적용되도록 추가했고, 새 문장은 소듐 누설과 그에 따른 화학 반응의 영향을 설계자가 고려하게 하려는 것이다.
