# Natrium seismic classification (Natrium Seismic Classification of SSCs)

> [!abstract] 요약
> `PSAR` Chapter 6은 safety-significant Structures, Systems, and Components%%t:SSC%%의 `seismic design bases`를 safety classification과 일관된 seismic classification 형태의 지진 special treatment%%t:SPECIAL_TREATMENT%%로 정한다. Safety-Related SSC%%t:SSC%%는 seismic risk significance에 따라 `SCS1`과 `SCS2`로, Non-Safety-Related with Special Treatment SSC%%t:SSC%%는 지진 위험 기여에 따라 `SCN1`–`SCN3`로 나뉘며, 초기 분류는 construction permit 단계에서 부여되고 operating license 단계를 위해 risk-informed 과정과 seismic PRA로 확인되거나 조정된다.

## 원문 사실
- "Safety-significant structures, systems, or components (SSCs) are designed to withstand the effects of natural phenomena including earthquakes in accordance with Principal Design Criterion (PDC) 2." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1 p.14]
- "The seismic design bases for SSCs are established by seismic special treatment in the form of seismic classifications that are consistent with the safety classification." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1 p.14]
- "Seismic design and analysis methodology and parameters presented in this section are applicable to safety-related (SR) and non-safety-related with special treatment (NSRST) SSCs." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1 p.14]
- "The seismic instrumentation required by 10 CFR Appendix S is provided by the Seismic Monitoring System, which is described in Section 1.1.4.3.16." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1 p.14]
- "Seismic classifications are defined to support graded application of seismic design requirements to SSCs and are initially assigned to SSCs based on their safety classification and the probabilistic risk assessment safety functions performed by the SSCs." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1.1 p.14]
- "Seismic classifications establish SSC seismic design requirements." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1.1 p.14]
- "The seismic classifications are SCS1 for SR seismic risk significant SSCs, SCS2 for SR non-seismic risk significant SSCs, and SCN1, SCN2, and SCN3 for NSRST SSCs based on contribution to seismic risk." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1.1 p.14]
- "SR SSCs are designed to withstand the effects of the safe shutdown earthquake (SSE) and remain functional, consistent with the requirements for Seismic Category I SSCs described in Regulatory Guide (RG) 1.29 Revision 6, “Seismic Design Classification for Nuclear Power Plants”." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1.1 p.14]
- "SR SSCs are assigned a seismic classification of SCS1 or SCS2 to provide graded levels of seismic special treatment based on seismic risk significance." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1.1 p.14]
- "An SCS1 classification imposes seismic requirements consistent with the Seismic Category I classification from RG 1.29, and an SCS2 classification provides for reduced seismic qualification requirements commensurate with the seismic risk significance of the SSC function." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1.1 p.14]
- "SCS1 is initially assigned to SR SSCs unless failure of the SR SSC in a seismic event does not adversely affect the safety significant function, in which case the SR SSC is assigned an SCS2 classification." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1.1 p.14]
- "Details on the seismic design and qualification requirements of the SCS1 and SCS2 seismic classifications are presented in Table 6.4-1A." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1.1 p.14]
- "NSRST SSCs are designed using commercial standards with additional seismic special treatments applied to provide confidence of functional performance following the SSE." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1.1 p.14]
- "Seismic special treatments for NSRST SSCs are developed based on application of ASCE/SEI 7-16 (Reference 6.4-2) to develop seismic demands and design requirements using a maximum considered earthquake that meets or exceeds the SSE." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1.1 p.14]
- "SCN1, SCN2, or SCN3 seismic classifications are assigned to NSRST SSCs to provide graded levels of seismic special treatment and associated seismic performance." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1.1 p.14]
- "Initial seismic classification for NSRST SSCs is based on various factors including SSC function, importance to life safety consistent with commercial building code, and potential contribution to events with dose consequences." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1.1 p.14]
- "Contribution to events with dose consequences is evaluated through a review of LBEs and an evaluation of potential unmitigated dose consequences from common cause seismic failure." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1.1 p.14]
- "These evaluations are taken to provide an initial determination of potential contribution to seismic risk." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1.1 p.14]
- "Details on the seismic design and qualification requirements of the SCN1, SCN2, and SCN3 seismic classifications are presented in Table 6.4-1B." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1.1 p.14]
- "SCN3 is assigned as a minimum initial seismic classification for NSRST SSCs and the initial seismic classification is increased to SCN2 or SCN1 as appropriate based on the factors above." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1.1 p.14]
- "SCN1 should be assigned to impose seismic qualification for active components, SCN2 or SCN1 are assigned as appropriate to meet minimum building code requirements, and SCN1 is assigned for SSCs identified as having a high potential contribution to seismic risk." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1.1 p.14]
- "Initial seismic classifications are assigned at the CP stage and will be confirmed or adjusted for the OL stage based on the risk-informed process and use of a seismic PRA." [PSAR_ch06 §6.4.1.1 p.15]

## 해설
safety-significant SSC%%t:SSC%%는 principal design criteria%%t:PDC%% 2에 따라 지진을 포함한 자연 현상의 영향을 견디도록 설계된다. 이 절의 지진 설계·해석 방법과 변수는 Safety-Related%%t:SR%%과 Non-Safety-Related with Special Treatment%%t:NSRST%% SSC%%t:SSC%%에 적용되며, `10 CFR Appendix S`가 요구하는 `seismic instrumentation`은 Seismic Monitoring System이 제공한다. seismic classification은 지진 설계 요건의 `graded application`을 위해 정의되고, SSC%%t:SSC%%의 safety classification과 SSC%%t:SSC%%가 수행하는 PRA Safety Function%%t:PSF%%에 따라 처음 부여된다.

SR%%t:SR%% SSC%%t:SSC%%는 Safe Shutdown Earthquake%%t:SSE%%의 영향을 견디고 기능을 유지하도록 `RG 1.29 Revision 6`의 `Seismic Category I` 요건과 일관되게 설계된다. `SCS1`은 SR%%t:SR%% seismic risk significant SSC%%t:SSC%%, `SCS2`는 SR%%t:SR%% non-seismic risk significant SSC%%t:SSC%%에 부여된다. `SCS1`은 `Seismic Category I`과 일관된 요건을 부과하고 `SCS2`는 SSC%%t:SSC%% 기능의 seismic risk significance에 맞게 줄어든 qualification 요건을 둔다. SR%%t:SR%% SSC%%t:SSC%%에는 처음에 `SCS1`을 부여하되, 지진에서 그 SSC%%t:SSC%%의 고장이 safety significant function에 악영향을 주지 않으면 `SCS2`를 부여한다. `Table 6.4-1A`에서 두 분류는 모두 `Seismic Demand`가 Safe Shutdown Earthquake%%t:SSE%% 이상이고 설계 코드가 `RG 1.29`의 `SC-I` SSC%%t:SSC%%와 일관되지만, qualification은 `SCS1`이 `SC-I` SSC%%t:SSC%%와 일관된 기능의 `Reasonable assurance`, `SCS2`가 `ASCE 7`의 `commercial standards`와 일관된 기능의 `Reasonable confidence`이다.

NSRST%%t:NSRST%% SSC%%t:SSC%%는 Safe Shutdown Earthquake%%t:SSE%% 뒤 기능 성능에 대한 확신을 주도록 `commercial standards`에 추가 지진 special treatment%%t:SPECIAL_TREATMENT%%를 더해 설계된다. 그 special treatment%%t:SPECIAL_TREATMENT%%는 Safe Shutdown Earthquake%%t:SSE%% 이상인 maximum considered earthquake로 `ASCE/SEI 7-16`을 적용해 `seismic demands`와 설계 요건을 정한다. `SCN1`, `SCN2`, `SCN3`는 등급별 지진 special treatment%%t:SPECIAL_TREATMENT%%와 지진 성능을 주도록 부여된다. 초기 분류는 SSC%%t:SSC%% 기능, `commercial building code`와 일관된 `importance to life safety`, `events with dose consequences`에 대한 잠재 기여 등에 기반하며, 그 기여는 Licensing Basis Event%%t:LBE%% 검토와 `common cause seismic failure`의 `potential unmitigated dose consequences` 평가로 판단하고, 이 평가는 지진 위험에 대한 잠재 기여의 초기 판단으로 쓰인다. `SCN3`는 NSRST%%t:NSRST%% SSC%%t:SSC%%의 최소 초기 분류이고 위 요인에 따라 적절히(`as appropriate`) `SCN2`나 `SCN1`로 올린다. `active components`의 지진 qualification을 부과하려면 `SCN1`을 부여해야 하고(`should`), 최소 `building code` 요건을 만족하도록 적절히 `SCN2`나 `SCN1`을, 지진 위험 기여 가능성이 높은 SSC%%t:SSC%%에는 `SCN1`을 부여한다. `Table 6.4-1B`에서 세 분류 모두 `Maximum Considered Earthquake Ground Motion`이 Safe Shutdown Earthquake%%t:SSE%% 이상이고, `SCN1`은 `Seismic Risk Category IV`와 importance factor `1.5`, `SCN2`는 `Seismic Risk Category III`와 `1.25`, `SCN3`는 `1.0`이며, `SCN1`과 `SCN2`의 `Non-structural components`는 functional qualification이 필요한 designated seismic system이다. 초기 분류는 construction permit%%t:CP%% 단계에서 부여되고 operating license%%t:OL%% 단계에서 risk-informed 과정과 seismic PRA로 확인·조정된다.

지진 설계는 Safe Shutdown Earthquake%%t:SSE%%와 그 `one-third` 크기인 Operating Basis Earthquake의 `ground motion magnitudes`에 기반한다. NSRST%%t:NSRST%% SSC%%t:SSC%%의 `seismic demands`는 `ASCE/SEI 7-16`에 따라 정하고, `SCN1`–`SCN3`의 risk-targeted maximum considered earthquake 응답 변수는 Safe Shutdown Earthquake%%t:SSE%%와 부지 risk-targeted maximum considered earthquake 값을 모두 포괄한다. NSRST%%t:NSRST%% SSC%%t:SSC%%는 `commercial codes`로 설계되고, 그 maximum considered earthquake는 Safe Shutdown Earthquake%%t:SSE%% 이상이며 seismic risk category와 importance factor에 따라 조정된다.

NEI 18-04의 special treatment 표는 SR%%t:SR%% SSC%%t:SSC%%가 Safe Shutdown Earthquake%%t:SSE%% 뒤 Required Safety Function%%t:RSF%%를 수행해야 하고 NSRST%%t:NSRST%%와 Non-Safety-Related with No Special Treatment%%t:NST%% SSC%%t:SSC%%는 `Seismic II/I` 요건, 곧 SR%%t:SR%% SSC%%t:SSC%%의 RSF%%t:RSF%% 수행을 방해하지 않을 것을 만족해야 한다고 적고, 표의 범주별 적용은 일반 지침일 뿐 처방이 아니라고 적는다. `PSAR`은 NSRST%%t:NSRST%% SSC%%t:SSC%%에 기능 성능 확신을 위한 지진 special treatment%%t:SPECIAL_TREATMENT%%와 `SCN1`·`SCN2`의 functional qualification을 두고, SR%%t:SR%% SSC%%t:SSC%% 가운데 `SCS2`에는 `ASCE 7`의 `commercial standards`와 일관된 qualification을 둔다. `PSAR` Chapter 5의 risk-significant 기준은 Frequency-Consequence Target%%t:FC_TARGET%%과 세 cumulative risk target%%t:CUM_RISK_TARGET%%에 대한 것이며, `SCS1`과 `SCS2`를 가르는 seismic risk significance의 정량 기준은 vault에 없다.
