# NRC의 Natrium MST 방법론 평가 (NRC Evaluation of the Natrium Mechanistic Source Term Methodology)

> [!abstract] 요약
> NRC staff는 Kemmerer Unit 1 `PSAR`이 승인된 방법론 NAT-9392-A로 Mechanistic Source Term%%t:MST%%을 만들었고, construction permit%%t:CP%% 단계에서 MST%%t:MST%% 해석이 적절하다고 판단했다. 다만 Design Basis Accident%%t:DBA%% 해석과 non-DBA 해석의 user input이 서로 맞지 않아 NAT-9392-A의 L&C 6을 완전히 충족했다고 보지 않았고, user input과 가정의 검토를 operating license%%t:OL%% 단계로 넘겼다. 사건별 선원항(source term)%%t:SOURCE_TERM%%은 사업자가 설계가 성숙하면서 개발할 것으로 staff가 예상한다고 적었다.

## 원문 사실
- "The PSAR incorporates by reference sections 1 through 8 of the TR NAT-9392-A, which describes the methodology to develop event-specific MSTs." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.187]
- "PSAR tables 3.2-1 through 3.2-18 provide the 18 source terms used in the radiological consequence analyses of LBEs with release and DBAs." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.189]
- "There are eight L&Cs on the use of NAT-9392-A." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.187]
- "The staff determined that the use of NAT-9392-A in the PSAR meets TR L&Cs 1 through 3 because the preliminary Natrium design described in the PSAR is consistent with the design information in the L&Cs." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.188]
- "Consistent with L&C 4, the staff verified through review of the LBE analyses in PSAR chapter 3 that the scenarios in which the MST methodology is used do not include sodium bulk boiling." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.188]
- "The staff determined that the code qualification, verification and validation activities discussed in NAT-9392-A for this methodology are sufficient for preliminary analyses supporting a CP application and thus TR L&C 5 is addressed." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.188]
- "Although the documentation provided justification of user inputs and assumptions for the preliminary analyses, the staff noted that the DBA and non-DBA analyses were inconsistent with each other." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.188]
- "Therefore, the staff could not find that TR L&C 6 is fully met at this time but determined it is reasonable to leave further review of the user inputs and assumptions in the staff’s evaluation of the implementation of NAT-9392-A using final design information in support of an OL application." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.188]
- "Because the PSAR summarizes how the NEI 18-04 LMP methodology is implemented for preliminary safety analyses for the Natrium design, the staff determined that the application meets TR L&C 7." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.188]
- "The staff determined that TR L&C 8 is addressed because the activities described in the TR are not needed because the source term EM is sufficient for preliminary analysis" [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.188]
- "The staff observed in the audit that the development of source terms was inconsistent when the RADTRAD code was used with conservative assumptions for the DBAs when compared to using the ]] and more detailed models (e.g., for pool scrubbing) for the non-DBA LBEs." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.191]
- "The staff also noted that although the same base information was referenced" [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.191]
- "]], the analyses used different user input based on those sources." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.191]
- "In audit discussions, the applicant acknowledged these inconsistencies and stated that as they develop the scenario-specific source terms to support the OL application, they anticipate resolving the differences and providing the necessary justifications." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.191]
- "In response to these audit discussions, USO provided supplemental information by letter dated September 17, 2025 (ML25260A002). In this letter USO states:" [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.191]
- "Additionally, mechanistic source term evaluation model treatment of sodium pool scrubbing and iodine releases will be further examined for the OL stage to confirm the implementation and supporting methodology is appropriate for the application." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.191]
- "Given the preliminary nature of the reactor design and analyses for the CP application, the staff did not find it necessary to perform independent event progression and source term analyses with a comprehensive system analysis code to evaluate the PSAR." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.190]
- "However, using information from the PSAR and the supporting MST analyses available in the audit, the staff performed calculations on the LBE MSTs to ascertain the effective radionuclide retention within the credited barriers for the event." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.190]
- "This included estimation of the effective removal rate of each of the radionuclide transport and removal phenomena within those barriers, such as bubble scrubbing in the sodium pool, SFP scrubbing, aerosol natural deposition, and filtration systems." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.190]
- "The staff observed through audit that the MST analyses used radionuclide inventories for the sources of radiological material at risk of release (MAR) which were estimated using the methods described in the NAT-9392-A methodology." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.190]
- "The staff found the audited information showed that the MAR isotopic inventories were conservatively estimated using the NAT-9392-A methodology and supported the PSAR section 3.2 description of the source term development for LBEs." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.190]
- "The staff confirmed through the regulatory audit that some LBE consequence analyses applied a representative source term based on another related scenario, sometimes with source term scaling factors to account for different LBE conditions. Based on its review, the staff determined that the use of representative source terms is reasonable for the preliminary analyses." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.189]
- "The staff anticipates that the applicant will develop LBE-specific source terms as the design and analyses mature and that the final safety analysis report (FSAR) in support of the OL application will include these LBE-specific source terms in the consequence analyses instead of carrying forward the PSAR analyses." - "The assumptions made on the barrier leakage rates in the MST analyses to support the FSAR will inform the development of the Technical Specification 3.6 “Functional Containment” limiting conditions for operation and surveillance requirements for the OL stage, as listed in PSAR table 11.5-1." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.192]
- "Accordingly, based on its review of MST information in the PSAR and docketed supplement, as confirmed through audit, the staff determined the MST analyses are adequate at the CP stage. The staff will review updated MST analyses as part of the OL application." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.1 p.191]
- "Therefore, the staff determined that the use of the MSTs described in the PSAR supports the issuance of a CP pursuant to the regulations of 10 CFR 50.34(a)(1), (4), and 10 CFR 50.35, as applicable." [SER_KU1_CP §3.2.2 p.210]

## 해설
NRC staff는 `PSAR`이 참조로 포함한 NAT-9392-A의 L&C 8개를 하나씩 검토했다.
- L&C 1–3은 예비 설계가 L&C의 설계 정보와 맞으므로 충족되었다.
- L&C 4는 MST%%t:MST%% 방법론이 쓰인 시나리오에 소듐 bulk boiling이 없음을 확인해 충족되었다.
- L&C 5와 8은 예비 해석에는 충분하다는 이유로 처리되었다.
- L&C 7은 `PSAR`이 NEI 18-04 Licensing Modernization Project%%t:LMP%% 방법론의 적용을 요약하므로 충족되었다.
- L&C 6만 완전히 충족되었다고 보지 않았다. DBA%%t:DBA%% 해석과 non-DBA 해석이 서로 맞지 않았기 때문이며, user input과 가정의 검토는 최종 설계 정보로 OL%%t:OL%% 단계에서 하기로 했다.

불일치의 내용은 다음과 같다. DBA%%t:DBA%%에는 보수적 가정의 RADTRAD를 썼고, non-DBA Licensing Basis Event%%t:LBE%%에는 pool scrubbing%%t:POOL_SCRUBBING%% 등을 더 상세히 다루는 모델을 썼으며, 같은 출처에 근거하면서 user input이 달랐다. 사업자 쪽(`USO`)은 2025-09-17 보충 서한에서 pool scrubbing%%t:POOL_SCRUBBING%%과 요오드 방출 처리를 OL%%t:OL%% 단계에서 다시 검토하겠다고 밝혔다. 서한 날짜는 원문 사실의 "September 17, 2025"이다.

staff는 독립적인 사고 진행 해석은 하지 않았다. 대신 `PSAR`과 audit 자료로 credit된 barrier%%t:BARRIER%% 안의 실효 보유량을 계산했다. 여기에는 소듐 풀 bubble scrubbing, `SFP` scrubbing, 에어로졸 자연 침착, 여과의 실효 제거율 추정이 포함된다. material at risk%%t:MATERIAL_AT_RISK%% 재고는 NAT-9392-A 방법으로 보수적으로 추정되었다고 판단했다. 대표 선원항%%t:SOURCE_TERM%%을 다른 사건에 쓰거나 배율을 적용한 것도 예비 해석으로는 합리적이라고 보았다. 다만 OL%%t:OL%% 신청의 `FSAR`에는 LBE%%t:LBE%%별 선원항%%t:SOURCE_TERM%%이 들어갈 것으로 예상했다.

barrier%%t:BARRIER%% 누설률은 예비 설계 정보나 보수적 가정에 근거한다. `FSAR` 해석에 쓰일 누설률 가정은 OL%%t:OL%% 단계 Technical Specification 3.6 "Functional Containment"의 운전제한조건과 감시요건 개발에 반영된다. staff의 결론은 MST%%t:MST%% 해석이 CP%%t:CP%% 단계에서 적절하고 CP%%t:CP%% 발급을 뒷받침한다는 것이다.
